**5\-TADDHITA-KAṆḌA**

**Apacca-taddhita**  
	  
Atha nāmato eva vibhatyantā apaccādi-atthavisese taddhituppattī ti nāmato paraṃ taddhitavidhānam ārabhīyate.[^1]

	Tattha tasmā tividhaliṅgato paraṃ hutvā hitā sahitā ti **taddhitā**, **ṇā**dipaccayānam etaṃ adhivacanaṃ, tesaṃ vā nāmikānaṃ hitā upakārā taddhitā ti anvatthabhūtaparasamaññāvasenā pi **ṇā**dippaccayā ’va taddhitā nāma.

	“Vasiṭṭhassa apaccan” ti viggahe.

	“Liṅgañ ca nipaccate” ti ito **liṅga**ggahaṇam anuvattate.

	**361\. Vā ṇa ’pacce** (752) \[III 1\].

	Chaṭṭhiyantato liṅgamhā **ṇa**ppaccayo hoti vikappena “tassa apaccam” icc etasmiṃ atthe.

	So ca.

	**362\. Dhātu-liṅgehi parā paccayā** (905).

	Dhātūhi, liṅgehi ca paccayā parā ’va hontī ti paribhāsato apaccatthasambandhiliṅgato[^2] chaṭṭhiyantā yeva paro hoti. Paṭicca etasmā attho etī ti **paccayo**, patīyanti anena atthā ti vā paccayo, “vuttatthānam appayogo” ti **apacca**saddassa appayogo, “tesaṃ vibhattiyo lopā ce” ti vibhattilopo ca.

	**363\. Tesaṃ ṇo lopaṃ** (834).

	Tesaṃ taddhitapaccayānaṃ **ṇā**nubandhānaṃ **ṇ**akāro lopam āpajjate.

	**364\. Vuddh’ ādisarassa vā ’saṃyogantassa saṇe ca** (834).

	Ādisarassa vā ādibyañjanassa vā asaṃyogantassa vuddhi hoti sa**ṇ**akārappaccaye pare. Saṃyogo anto assā ti saṃyoganto, tadañño **asaṃyoganto**. Atha vā vavatthitavibhāsattho ’yaṃ **vā**saddo, tadā ca pakatibhūte liṅge sarānam ādisarassa asaṃyogantassa vuddhi hoti sa**ṇ**akāre taddhitappaccaye pare ti attho.  
	Tena:

		Vāsiṭṭhādīsu niccā ’yaṃ, anicc’ oḷumpikādīsu[^3];  
		Na vuddhi nīlapītādo[^4], vavatthitavibhāsato.[^5]

	**Ca**saddaggahaṇam avadhāraṇatthaṃ.

	Tassā vuddhiyā aniyamappasaṅge niyamatthaṃ paribhāsam āha.

	**365\. A-y-uvaṇṇānañ c’ ā-y-o vuddhī** (864).

	Tesaṃ **a**kāra-**i**vaṇṇ’-**u**vaṇṇānam eva yathākkamaṃ **ā e o** icc ete vuddhiyo honti. **Ca**saddaggahaṇam avuddhisampiṇḍanatthaṃ, avadhāraṇatthaṃ vā. I ca u ca yu, yu eva vaṇṇā yuvaṇṇā, a ca yuvaṇṇā ca **a-y-uvaṇṇā**. Ā ca e ca o ca **āyo**. Puna **vuddhi**ggahaṇaṃ “negamajānapadā” ti ādīsu uttarapadavuddhibhāvatthaṃ, ettha ca “**a-y-u**vaṇṇānan” ti ṭhānaniyamavacanaṃ āyonaṃ vuddhibhāvappasaṅganivattanatthaṃ.

		Yathā hi katavuddhīnaṃ, puna vuddhi na hoti ’ha;  
		Tathā sabhāvavuddhīnaṃ, āyonaṃ puna vuddhi na.

	Tato **a**kārassa **ā**kāro vuddhi, “saralopo ’mādesa-ppaccayādimhi saralope tu pakatī”[^6] ti saralopapakatibhāvā, “naye paraṃ yutte”[^7] ti paranayanaṃ katvā taddhitattā “taddhitasamāsa”  icc ādinā nāmabyapadese kate pure viya **syā**dyuppatti.

	Vāsiṭṭho, Vasiṭṭhassa putto vā, Vāsiṭṭhā icc ādi **purisa**saddasamaṃ. Tassa nattupanattādayo pi tadupacārato Vāsiṭṭhā yeva. Evaṃ sabbattha gottataddhite paṭhamappakatito yeva paccayo hoti.

	**Itthiyaṃ** **ṇa**ppaccayantattā “ṇava-ṇika-ṇeyya-ṇa-ntūhī” ti **Vāsiṭṭha**saddato **ī**paccayo. Saralopādiṃ katvā itthippaccayantattā “taddhitasamāsa” icc ādisutte **ca**ggahaṇena nāmabyapadese kate **syā**dyuppatti, Vāsiṭṭhī kaññā, Vāsiṭṭhī, Vāsiṭṭhiyo icc ādi **itthi**saddasamaṃ.

	**Napuṃsake** Vāsiṭṭhaṃ apaccaṃ, Vāsiṭṭhāni apaccāni icc ādi **citta**saddasamaṃ. Evaṃ upari pi taddhitantassa tiliṅgatā veditabbā.

	Bhāradvājassa putto Bhāradvājo,[^8] Vesāmittassa putto Vesāmitto,[^9] Gotamassa putto Gotamo.[^10] Ettha ca ayuvaṇṇattābhāvā **ā**kārādīnaṃ na vuddhi hoti. Vasudevassa apaccaṃ Vāsudevo,[^11] Bāladevo. “Cittako” ti ādīsu pana saṃyogantattā vuddhi na bhavati.

	“Vacchassa apaccan” ti viggahe **ṇa**mhi sampatte “vā ṇa ’pacce” ti ito **vā** ti taddhitavidhāne sabbattha vattate, tena sabbattha vākyavuttiyo bhavanti. “Apacce” ti padaṃ yāva **saṃsaṭṭha**ggahaṇā ’va vattate.

	**366\. Ṇāyana-ṇāna Vacchādito** (754).

	**Vaccha Kicca** icc evamādito chaṭṭhiyantato gottagaṇato **ṇayana ṇāna** icc ete paccayā honti vā “tass’ āpaccam” icc etasmiṃ atthe. Sabbattha **ṇ**akārānubandho vuddhattho, sesaṃ pubbasamaṃ, saṃyogantattā vuddhi-abhāvo ’va viseso.

	Vacchāyano Vacchāno[^12] Vacchassa putto vā. Evaṃ Kaccassa putto Kaccāyano Kaccāno,[^13] Moggallassa putto Moggallāyano Moggallāno.[^14] Evaṃ Aggivessāyano Aggivessāno,[^15] Kaṇhāyano Kaṇhāno,[^16] Sākaṭāyano Sākaṭāno, Muñcāyano Muñcāno,[^17] Kuñjāyano Kuñjāno icc ādi. Ākatigaṇo ’yaṃ.

	“Kattikāya apaccan” ti viggahe.

	**367\. Ṇeyyo Kattikādīhi** (755) \[III 3\].

	**Ādi**saddo ’yaṃ pakāre vattate. **Kattikā Vinatā Rohiṇī** icc evamādīhi itthiyaṃ vattamānehi liṅgehi **ṇeyya**ppaccayo hoti vā “tass’ āpaccam” icc etasmiṃ atthe.

	Vibhattilope “pakati c’ assa sarantassā” ti pakatibhāvo. Kattikeyyo, Kattikāya putto vā. Evaṃ Vinatāya apaccaṃ Venateyyo,[^18] **i**kārass’ **e**kāro vuddhi, Rohiṇiyā putto Rohiṇeyyo,[^19] Gaṅgāya apaccaṃ Gaṅgeyyo, bhaginiyā putto bhāgineyyo,[^20] Nadiyā putto Nādeyyo. Evaṃ anteyyo, āheyyo, kāmeyyo.[^21] Suciyā apaccaṃ soceyyo, ettha **u**kārass’ **o**kāro vuddhi, bālāya apaccaṃ bāleyyo icc ādi.

	“Dakkhass’ āpaccan” ti viggahe **ṇa**mhi sampatte.

	**368\. Ato ṇi vā** (756) \[III 5\].

	**A**kārantato liṅgamhā **ṇi**paccayo hoti vā “tass’ āpaccam” icc etasmiṃ atthe. Dakkhi, Dakkhī, Dakkhayo, Doṇassa apaccaṃ Doṇi. Evaṃ Vāsavi, Sakyaputti, Nāṭaputti, Dāsaputti, Dāsarathi,[^22] Vāruṇi, Kaṇḍi, Bāladevi, Pāvaki, Jinadattassa apaccaṃ Jenadatti, Suddhodani, Anuruddhi icc ādi.

	Puna **vā**ggahaṇena apaccatthe **ṇika**ppaccayo.[^23] **Aditi**\-ādito **ṇya**ppaccayo ca. Yathā: Sakyaputtassa putto Sakyaputtiko, “tesu vuddhī” ti ādinā **ka**kārassa **ya**kāro,[^24] Sakyaputtiyo.[^25] Evaṃ Nāṭaputtiko, Jenadattiko, vimātuyā putto vemātiko.

	“Aditiyā putto” ti atthe **nya**ppaccayo, vuddhi ca.

	**369\. Avaṇṇo ye lopañ ca** (509). 

	**A**vaṇṇo taddhitabhūte **ya**ppaccaye pare lopam āpajjate. **Ca**saddena **i**vaṇṇo pī ti **i**kāralopo, “yavataṃ ta-la-na-dakārānaṃ byañjanāni ca-la-ña-ja-kā-rattan”[^26] ti  **tya**kārasaṃyogassa **ca**kāro, “paradvebhāvo ṭhāne”[^27] ti dvittaṃ, ādicco.[^28] Evaṃ Ditiyā putto Decco.[^29]

	“Kuṇḍaniyā putto” ti atthe **nya**ppacaye kate.

	“Kvac’ ādi-majjh’-uttaresū” ti vattate.

	**370\. Tesu vuddhi-lopāgama-vikāra-viparītādesā ca** (859) \[IV 126\].

	Tesu ādi-majjh’-uttaresu avihitalakkhaṇesu jinavacanānuparodhena kvaci vuddhi lopa-āgama-vikāra-viparīta-ādesā hontī ti saṃyogantatte pi ādivuddhi, **i**kāralope **nya**ssa **ñā**deso,[^30] Koṇḍañño,[^31] Kuruno putto Korabyo,[^32] etthā pi ten’ eva **u**kārassa **avā**deso, bhātuno putto bhātabyo.

	“Upagussa apaccan” ti viggahe.

	**371\. Ṇavo ’pagvādīhi** (757) \[III 1\].

	**Upagu manu** icc evamādīhi **u**kārantehi gottagaṇehi **ṇava**ppaccayo hoti vā “tass’ āpaccam” icc etasmiṃ atthe. **Ādi**saddassa c’ ettha pakāravācakattā **u**kārantato yevā ’yaṃ. Opagavo, opagavī, opagavaṃ, Manuno apaccaṃ mānavo, “mānuso” ti **ṇa**ppaccaye, **sā**game ca kate rūpaṃ,[^33] Bhagguno apaccaṃ Bhaggavo, Paṇḍuno apaccaṃ Paṇḍavo, Upavindussa apaccaṃ Opavindavo icc ādi.

	“Vidhavāya apaccan” ti atthe.

	**372\. Ṇera vidhavādito** (758-9) \[IV 3\].

	**Vidhavā**dito **ṇera**ppaccayo hoti vā apaccatthe. Vigato dhavo pati etissā ti vidhavā, vedhavero,[^34] bandhukiyā abhisāriṇiyā putto bandhukero, samaṇassa upajjhāyassa putto puttaṭṭhāniyattā ti sāmaṇero,[^35] nāḷikero icc ādi.

Apacca-taddhitaṃ

**Saṃsaṭṭhādi-anekattha-taddhita**

	“Tilena saṃsaṭṭhan” ti viggahe.

	**373\. Yena vā saṃsaṭṭhaṃ tarati carati vahati ṇiko** (764) \[IV 28-9\].

	Yena vā saṃsaṭṭhaṃ, yena vā tarati, yena vā carati, yena vā vahati, tato tatiyantato liṅgamhā tesu saṃsaṭṭhādīsv atthesu **ṇika**ppaccayo hoti vā. Telikaṃ bhojanaṃ, tilena abhisaṅkhatan ti attho. Telikī yāgu. Guḷena saṃsaṭṭhaṃ goḷikaṃ. Evaṃ ghātikaṃ, dādhikaṃ, māricikaṃ, loṇikaṃ.

	Nāvāya taratī ti nāviko,[^36] uḷumpena taratī ti oḷumpiko, vuddhi-ābhāvapakkhe uḷumpiko. Evaṃ kulliko, gopucchiko. Sakaṭena caratī ti sākaṭiko. Evaṃ pādiko, daṇḍiko, dhammena carati pavattatī ti dhammiko.[^37] Sīsena vahatī ti sīsiko, **vā**ggahaṇena **ī**kārassa vuddhi na hoti. Evaṃ aṃsiko, khandhiko, hatthiko, aṅguliko.

	Puna **vā**ggahaṇena aññatthesu pi **ṇika**ppaccayo, paradāraṃ gacchatī ti pāradāriko,[^38] pathaṃ gacchatī ti pathiko.

	“Vinayam adhīte,” “aveccā ’dhīte” ti vā viggahe.

	“Ṇiko” ti vattate.

	**374\. Tam adhīte tena-katādi sannidhāna-niyoga-sippa-bhaṇḍa-jīvikatthesu** **ca** (764) \[IV 28\].

	Catuppadam idaṃ. Tam adhīte ti atthe, tena katādīsv atthesu ca tamhi sannidhāno, tattha niyutto, tam assa sippaṃ, tam assa bhaṇḍaṃ, tam assa jīvikā icc etesv atthesu ca dutiyādivibhatyantehi liṅgehi **ṇika**ppaccayo hoti vā. Venayiko.[^39] Evaṃ suttantiko, ābhidhammiko.[^40]

	“Byākaraṇam adhīte” ti atthe **ṇika**ppaccayādimhi kate.

	“Vuddhādisarassa vā saṃyogantassa saṇe cā” ti vattamāne.

	**375\. Mā y-ūnam āgamo ṭhāne** (848).

	**I u** icc etesaṃ ādisarānaṃ asaṃyogantānaṃ mā vuddhi hoti saṇe, tatr’ eva vuddhi āgamo hoti ca ṭhāne ti **e**kāravuddhāgamo.

		“Ṭhāne” ti vacanā c’ ettha, **y-ū**nam ādesabhūtato;  
		**Ya-ve**hi pubb’ eva e-ovuddhiyo honti āgamā.

	Yakārassa dvibhāvo.

	Veyyākaraṇiko,[^41] nyāyam adhite ti neyyāyiko.[^42] Evaṃ takkiko, vediko, nemittiko.  
Kāyena kato payogo kāyiko, kāyena kataṃ kammaṃ kāyikaṃ,[^43] vacasā kataṃ kammaṃ vācasikaṃ. Evaṃ mānasikaṃ, ettha ca “sa sare v’ āgamo”[^44] ti sutte vavatthita**vā**saddena paccaye pare pi **sā**gamo, therehi katā saṅgīti therikā.[^45] Evaṃ pañcasatikā,[^46] sattasatikā,[^47] ettha “ṇava-ṇikā” ‘disutte anuvattita**vā**ggahaṇena **ī**paccayo na hoti.

	**Sannidhānatthe** sarīre sannidhānā vedanā sārīrikā,[^48] sārīrikaṃ dukkhaṃ. Evaṃ mānasikā, mānasikaṃ.

	**Niyuttatthe** dvāre niyutto dovāriko,[^49] ettha “mā y-ūnam āgamo ṭhāne” ti **v**akārato pubbe **o**kārāgamo.[^50] Evaṃ bhaṇḍāgāriko,[^51] nāgariko, navakammiko,[^52] vanakammiko, ādikammiko,[^53] odariko, rathiko, pathiko, upāye niyutto opāyiko,[^54] cetasi niyuttā cetasikā.[^55]

	**Sippatthe** vīṇāvādanaṃ vīṇā; vīṇā assa sippaṃ veṇiko.[^56] Evaṃ pāṇaviko, modiṅgiko,[^57] vaṃsiko.

	**Bhaṇḍatthe** gandho assa bhaṇḍan ti gandhiko.[^58] Evaṃ teliko,[^59] goḷiko, pūviko, paṇṇiko, tambūliko, loṇiko.

	**Jīvikatthe** urabbhaṃ hantvā jīvati, urabbham assa jīvikā ti vā orabbhiko.[^60] Evaṃ māgaviko,[^61] ettha **va**kārāgamo. Sūkariko,[^62] sākuṇiko,[^63] macchiko icc ādi.

	“Tena-katādī” ti ettha **ādi**ggahaṇena tena hataṃ, tena baddhaṃ, tena kītaṃ, tena dibbati, so assa āvudho, so assa ābādho, tattha pasanno, tassa santakaṃ, tam assa parimāṇaṃ, tassa rāsi, taṃ arahati, tam assa sīlaṃ, tattha jāto, tattha vasati, tatra vidito, tadatthāya saṃvattati, tato āgato, tato sambhūto, tad assa payojanan ti evam ādi-atthe ca **ṇika**ppaccayo hoti. Yathā: jālena hato, hanatī ti vā jāliko.[^64] Evaṃ bāḷisiko,[^65] vākariko,[^66] suttena baddho suttiko, varattāya baddho vārattiko nāgo.

	Vatthena kītaṃ bhaṇḍaṃ vattikaṃ.[^67] Evaṃ kumbhikaṃ, phālikaṃ, sovaṇṇikaṃ, sātikaṃ. Akkhena dibbatī ti akkhiko.[^68] Evaṃ sālākiko, tindukiko, ambaphaliko. Cāpo assa āvudho ti cāpiko.[^69] Evaṃ tomariko, muggariko, mosaliko.

	Vāto assa ābādho ti vātiko. Evaṃ semhiko, pittiko.

	Buddhe pasanno buddhiko. Evaṃ dhammiko saṃghiko. Buddhassa santako buddhiko.[^70] Evaṃ dhammiko, saṃghiko[^71] vihāro, saṃghikā bhūmi. Saṃghikaṃ cīvaraṃ, puggalikaṃ.[^72]

	Kumbho assa parimāṇan ti kumbhikaṃ.[^73] Evaṃ khārikaṃ, doṇikaṃ. Kumbhassa rāsi kumbhiko. Kumbhaṃ arahatī ti kumbhiko.[^74] Evaṃ doṇiko, aṭṭhamāsiko, kahāpaṇiko, āsītikā[^75] gāthā, nāvutikā,[^76] sātikaṃ, sāhassikaṃ.[^77] Sandiṭṭham arahatī ti sandiṭṭhiko,[^78] “ehi passā” ti imaṃ vidhiṃ arahatī ti ehipassiko.[^79]

	**Sīlatthe** paṃsukūladhāraṇaṃ paṃsukūlaṃ, taṃ sīlam assā ti paṃsukūliko.[^80] Evaṃ tecīvariko,[^81] ekāsane bhojanasīlo ekāsaniko,[^82] rukkhamūle vasanasīlo rukkhamūliko,[^83] tathā āraññiko,[^84] sosāniko.[^85]

	**Jātatthe** apāye jāto āpāyiko.[^86] Evaṃ nerayiko,[^87] sāmuddiko[^88] maccho, vassesu jāto vassiko, vassikā, vassikaṃ pupphaṃ, sāradiko, hemantiko, vāsantiko, cātuddasiko,[^89] Rājagahe jāto, Rājagahe vasatī ti vā Rājagahiko[^90] jano, Magadhesu jāto, vasatī ti vā Māgadhiko, Māgadhikā, Māgadhikaṃ, Sāvatthiyaṃ jāto, vasatī ti vā Sāvatthiko, Kāpilavatthiko, Vesāliko.[^91]

	Loke vidito lokiko,[^92] lokāya saṃvattatī ti pi lokiko.[^93] Tathā mātito āgataṃ mātikaṃ,[^94] pitito āgataṃ pettikaṃ nāmaṃ.

	**Sambhūtatthe** mātito sambhūtaṃ mattikaṃ.[^95] Evaṃ pettikaṃ.[^96] Upadhi tassa payojanaṃ opadhikaṃ.[^97]

	**Sakatthe pi** asaṅkhāro yeva asaṅkhārikaṃ.[^98] Evaṃ sasaṅkhārikaṃ,[^99] nāmam eva nāmikaṃ. Evaṃ ākhyātikaṃ, opasaggikaṃ, nepātikaṃ, catumahārāje bhatti etesan ti cātumahārājikā.[^100] Evaṃ aññatthe pi yojetabbaṃ.

	“Kasāvena rattan” ti viggahe.

	**376\. Ṇa rāgā tena rattaṃ tass’ edam aññatthesu ca** (765) \[IV 11, 34\]. 

	Rāgatthavācakā liṅgamhā “tena rattam” icc etasmiṃ atthe, “tassa” ti chaṭṭhiyantato “idam” icc etasmiṃ atthe ca aññatthesu ca **ṇa**ppaccayo hoti vā.

	Kāsāvaṃ[^101] vatthaṃ. Evaṃ kāsāyaṃ,[^102] kusumbhena rattaṃ kosumbhaṃ, haliddiyā rattaṃ hāliddaṃ, pattaṅgaṃ, mañjiṭṭhaṃ,[^103] kuṅkumaṃ, nīlena rattaṃ nīlaṃ. Evaṃ pītaṃ.

	**Idamatthe** mahiṃsassa idaṃ māhiṃsaṃ maṃsaṃ, dadhi-sappi-cammādikaṃ vā, sūkarassa idaṃ sūkaraṃ, Kaccāyanassa idaṃ Kaccāyanaṃ byākaraṇaṃ. Evaṃ sogataṃ sāsanaṃ.

	“Isissa idan” ti atthe **ṇa**ppaccaye kate vuddhimhi sampatte.

	“Saṇe,” “y-ūnam āgamo ṭhāne” ti ca vattate.

	**377\. Āttañ ca** (857).

	**I u** icc etesaṃ ādisarānaṃ **ā**ttañ ca hoti sa**ṇa**kārappaccaye pare, **ca**saddena **ri**kārāgamo ca ṭhāne ti **i**kārassa **ā**ttaṃ.

			Ṭhānādhikārato āttaṃ, is’-ūsabha-ujādinaṃ;  
			Isissa tu rikārāgamo c’ āttānantare bhave.

	Ārisyaṃ,[^104] usabhassa idaṃ āsabhaṃ[^105] ṭhānaṃ, āsabhī vācā.[^106]

	**Aññattha**ggahaṇena pana avidūrabhavo, tatra bhavo, tatra jāto, tato āgato, so assa nivāso, tassa issaro, Kattikādīhi niyutto māso, s’ āssa devatā, tam aveccā ’dhīte, tassa visayo deso, tasmiṃ dese atthi, tena nibbattaṃ, taṃ arahati, tassa vikāro, tam assa parimāṇan ti icc evamādīsv atthesu ca **ṇa**ppaccayo. Yathā: vidisāya avidūre bhavo Vediso[^107] gāmo, udumbarassa avidūre bhavaṃ odumbaraṃ vimānaṃ.

	**Bhavatthe** manasi bhavaṃ mānasaṃ[^108] sukhaṃ, **sā**gamo. Sare bhavo sāraso sakuṇo, sārasā sakuṇī, sārasaṃ pupphaṃ, urasi bhavo oraso[^109] putto, urasi saṃvaḍḍhitattā, mitte bhavā mettā,[^110] mettī vā, pure bhavā porī[^111] vācā.

	**Jātādīsu** pāvuse jāto pāvuso megho, pāvusā ratti, pāvusaṃ abbhaṃ, sarade jāto sārado māso,[^112] sāradā ratti, sāradaṃ pupphaṃ. Evaṃ sisiro, hemanto, vasanto, gimho, Mathurāyaṃ jāto Māthuro jano, Māthurā gaṇikā, Māthuraṃ vatthaṃ. Mathurāya āgato Māthuro, Mathurā assa nivāso ti Māthuro, Mathurāya  issaro Māthuro rājā. “Sabbato ko” ti ettha puna **sabbato**ggahaṇena taddhitato pi kvaci sasara**ka**kārāgamo, Māthurako vā, Rājagahe jāto, Rājagahā āgato, Rājagaho assa nivāso ti vā, Rājagahassa issaro ti vā Rājagaho, Rājagahako vā. “Yad anupapannā nipātanā sijjhantī”[^382] ti vattate.

**417\. Sakanāmehi** (833).

Yāsaṃ pana saṅkhyānaṃ aniddiṭṭhanāmadheyyānaṃ yāni rūpāni, tāni sakehi nāmehi nipaccante. Satasahassānaṃ sataṃ koṭi, itthiliṅgā, ekavacanantā ca, vaggabhede bahuvacanañ ca bhavati, koṭisatasahassānaṃ sataṃ pakoṭi, pakoṭisatasahassānaṃ sataṃ koṭippakoṭi. Evaṃ nahutaṃ, ninnahutaṃ, akkhobhinī, bindu, abbudaṃ, nirabbudaṃ, ahahaṃ, ababaṃ, aṭaṭaṃ, sogandhikaṃ, uppalaṃ, kumudaṃ, puṇḍarīkaṃ, padumaṃ, kathānaṃ, mahākathānaṃ, asaṅkheyyan ti.

		Icc evaṃ ṭhānato ṭhānaṃ, satalakkhaguṇaṃ mataṃ;  
		Koṭippabhutinaṃ vīsa-saṅkhyānañ ca yathākkamaṃ.

“Dve parimāṇani etassā” ti viggahe.

**418\. Dvadito ko ’nekatthe ca** (831).

**Dvi** icc evamādito gaṇanato **ka**ppaccayo hoti anekatthe. Dviko rāsi dvikaṃ. Evaṃ tikaṃ, catukkaṃ, pañcakaṃ, chakkaṃ, sattakaṃ, aṭṭhakaṃ, navakaṃ, dasakaṃ, paṇṇāsakaṃ, satakaṃ, sahassakaṃ icc ādi.

Saṅkhyā-taddhitaṃ.

**Abyaya-taddhita**

“Ekasmiṃ vāre bhuñjati,” “dvivāre bhuñjatī” ti viggahe.

**419\. Ekādito sakissa kkhattuṃ** (1282-4) \[IV 114\].

**Eka dvi ti** icc evamādito gaṇanato sakissa ṭhāne vāratthe **kkhattuṃ**paccayo hoti. Ekakkhattuṃ, dvikkhattuṃ bhuñjati, “sabbāsam āvuso”[^383] ti ādinā **si**lopo. Evaṃ tikkhattuṃ, catukkhattuṃ, pañcakkhattuṃ, chakkhattuṃ, sattakkhattuṃ, aṭṭhakkhattuṃ, navakkhattuṃ, dasakkhattuṃ, satakkhattuṃ, sahassakkhattuṃ,[^384] bahukkhattuṃ, katikkhattuṃ.[^385]

“Ekena vibhāgenā” ti viggahe.

Maṇḍūkagatiyā **saṅkhyā**ggahaṇam anuvattate.

**420\. Vibhāge dhā ca** (836) \[IV 110\].

Vibhāgatthe ekādisaṅkhyāto **dhā**paccayo hoti. **Ca**saddena **eka-dvi**to **jjha** ca,[^386] **suttā**dito **so** ca.[^387] Ekadhā. Dvīhi vibhāgehi dvidhā, dudhā vā, dvedhā. Tīhi vibhāgehi tidhā, tedhā vā, “tesu vuddhī” ti ādinā **i**kārass’ ekāro. Evaṃ catudhā, pañcadhā, chadhā, sattadhā, aṭṭhadhā, navadhā, dasadhā, satadhā, sahassadhā, katidhā, bahudhā.

**Jjha**ppaccaye ekadhā karotī ti ekajjhaṃ.[^388] Evaṃ dvejjhaṃ.[^389]

**So**paccaye suttena vibhāgena suttaso.[^390] Evaṃ byañjanaso,[^391] padaso,[^392] atthaso, bahuso, sabbākārena sabbaso,[^393] upāyaso, hetuso,[^394] ṭhānaso,[^395] yoniso.[^396]

**421\. Sabbanāmehi pakāravacane tu thā** (844) \[IV 108\].

Sabbanāmehi pakāravacanatthe **thā**paccayo hoti, **tu**saddena **thattā**paccayo ca. Sāmaññassa bhedako viseso pakāro, tass’ ābhidhāne ti attho, so pakāro tathā, taṃ pakāraṃ tathā, tena pakārena tathā, yena pakārena yathā. Evaṃ sabbathā, aññathā, itarathā, ubhayathā, **thattā**paccaye tena pakārena tathattā. Evaṃ yathattā, aññathattā.

Ko pakāro ti atthe.

**422\. Kim imehi thaṃ** (845) \[IV 109\].

**Kiṃ ima** icc etehi **thaṃ**paccayo hoti pakāravacanatthe. “Kissa ka ve cā”[^397] ti ettha **ca**saddena **ki**ssa **k**ādeso. Kathaṃ,[^398] kaṃ pakāraṃ kathaṃ, kena pakārena kathaṃ, ayaṃ pakāro itthaṃ,[^399] imaṃ pakāraṃ itthaṃ. Anena pakārena itthaṃ, “imass’ i thaṃ-dāni-ha-to-dhesu cā”[^400] ti **ima**saddassa **i**kāro, **dvi**ttaṃ. Ettha hi **kkhattuṃ**\-ādi-**thaṃ**pariyosānappaccayantānaṃ abyayataddhitattā nāmabyapadesaṃ katvā vibhattimhi kate “sabbāsam āvuso”[^401] ti ādinā vibhattilopo, “kvaci to pañcamyatthe”[^402] ti ādinā vutta-**to**\-ādippaccayantā ca idh’ eva abyayataddhite saṅgayhanti.

**423\. Yad anupapannā nipātanā sijjhanti** (830).

Ye saddā lakkhaṇena anupapannā aniddiṭṭhalakkhaṇā akkharādito, nāmopasagga-nipātato vā samāsataddhitādito vā, te nipātanā sijjhanti.

Taddhitato tāva:

	**Ima**smā **jja** siyā kāle, **samānāpara**to **jju** ca;  
	**Ima**saddass’ **a**kāro ca, **samāna**ssa ca **so** siyā.[^403]

Imasmiṃ kāle, imasmiṃ divase vā ajja,[^404] samāne kāle sajju, aparasmiṃ divase aparajju. Nipātehi **bhavatthe** **tana**ppaccayo.[^405] Ajja bhavaṃ ajjatanaṃ,[^406] ajja bhavā ajjatanī, sve bhavaṃ svātanaṃ.[^407] Evaṃ purātanaṃ,[^408] hiyyo bhavaṃ hiyyattanaṃ,[^409] hiyyo bhavā hiyyattanī icc ādi.

Abyaya-taddhitaṃ.

		Sāmaññavutti-bhāvatthābyayato taddhitaṃ tidhā;  
		Tatrādī catudhā ’paccānekatth’-assatthi-saṅkyāto.[^410]
