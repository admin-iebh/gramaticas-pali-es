	**671\. 681\. Hanādīhi ṇu-nu-tavo**[^48] (1317)**.**

**Hana jana bhā ri khanu ama ve dhe dha si ki hi** icc evamādīhi dhātūhi **ṇu nu tu** icc ete paccayā honti.

Bhojanaṃ hanati hiṃsati etenā ti haṇu, hanu (D. ii, 16\) vā, gamanaṃ janetī ti jāṇu (Vin. iii, 6), bhāti dibbatī ti bhāṇu (JA. iii, 57), nivāte riti gacchatī ti reṇu, khaṇitabbo avadāritabbo ti khāṇu (VinA. iii, 29), aṅgamaṅgassa rujjanabhāvaṃ vijjhanabhāvaṃ amati gacchatī ti aṇu (Khu. i, 54), veti tantasantāne bhavatī ti veṇu, bahisāre alan ti vā veṇu, dheti vacchaṃ pāyetī ti dhenu (VinA. i, 15), atthaṃ dhāretī ti dhātu (M. i, 73), gamanapacanādikaṃ kriyaṃ dhāreti vā dhātu (M. i, 73), sīyati bandhīyatī ti setu (A. i, 221), uddhaṃ gacchati pavattatī ti ketu, attano phalaṃ hinoti pavattatī ti hetu (Vin. i, 9).

There are ‘ṇu’, ‘nu’ and ‘tu’ after ‘hana’ and others.

There are the suffixes ‘ṇu’, ‘nu’ and ‘tu’ after the roots ‘hana’, ‘jana’, ‘bhā’, ‘ri’, ‘khanu’, ‘ama’, ‘ve’, ‘dhe’, ‘dha’, ‘si’, ‘ki’, ‘hi’ and others.

The food (bhojanaṃ) one hurts (hanati, hiṃsati) by this (etena), so (ti) jaw (haṇu, hanu) or (vā); going (gamanaṃ) it causes (janeti), so (ti) knee (jāṇu); it shines (bhāti, dibbati), so (ti) sun (bhāṇu); where there is no wind (nivāte) it goes (riti gacchati), so (ti) dust (reṇu); it is to be dug (khaṇitabbo, avadāritabbo), so (ti) tree stump (khāṇu); different parts of the body (aṅgamaṅgassa) paining (rujjanabhāvaṃ) piercing (vijjhanabhāvaṃ) it goes (amati, gacchati), so (ti) atomic particle (aṇu); it exists (veti, bhavati) in spreading of treads (tantasantāne), so (ti) bamboo (veṇu); outside core (bahisāre) fit (alan), so (ti) or (vā) bamboo (veṇu); it suckles (dheti, pāyeti) the calf (vacchaṃ), so (ti) cow (dhenu); meaning (atthaṃ) it holds (dhāreti), so (ti) root (dhātu); of going, cooking and so on (gamanapacanādikaṃ) action (kriyaṃ) it holds (dhāreti) or (vā) root (dhātu); it is to be united (sīyati, bandhīyati), so (ti) bridge (setu); upward (uddhaṃ) it exists (gacchati, pavattati), so (ti) flag, pinnacle (ketu); its own (attano) fruit (phalaṃ) it causes (hinoti, pavattati/pavatteti), so (ti) root (hetu).

	**672\. 682\. Kuṭādīhi ṭho** (1318)**.**

	**Kuṭa kusa kaṭa** icc evamādīhi dhātūhi, pāṭipadikehi ca **ṭha**paccayo hoti.

Aṅgamaṅgaṃ kuṭati chindatī ti kuṭṭhaṃ (Vin. iii, 131), dhaññena chādetabbo pūretabbo ti koṭṭho (D. i, 127), kaṭitabbaṃ madditabban ti kaṭṭhaṃ (Khu. v, 17).  
There is ‘ṭho’ after ‘kuṭa’ and others.

There is the suffix ‘ṭha’ after the stems and roots ‘kuṭa’, ‘kusa’, ‘kaṭa’ and others.

The different parts of the body (aṅgamaṅgaṃ) it cuts (kuṭati, chindati), so (ti) leprosy (kuṭṭhaṃ); with  grain (dhaññena) it is to be filled (chādetabbo, pūretabbo) so (ti) granary (koṭṭho); it is to be cut (kaṭitabbaṃ, madditabbaṃ), so (ti) timber (kaṭṭhaṃ).

	**673\. 683\. Manu-pūra-suṇādīhi ussa-nus’-isā** (1319)**.**

**Manu pūra suṇa ku su ila ala maha si ki** icc evamādīhi dhātūhi, pāṭipadikehi ca **ussa nusa isa** icc ete paccayā honti, puna nippajjante.

Kusalākusale dhamme manati jānātī ti manusso (Khu. vii, 9), mānuso (Khu. ii, 159). Kāraṇākāraṇaṃ manti jānātī ti vā manusso, mānusso. Atthānatthaṃ manati jānātī ti vā manusso, mānuso. Mātāpitūnaṃ hadayaṃ pūretī ti puriso (Khu. vii, 6), attano manorathaṃ pūretī ti vā puriso, pūretī ti vā poso (Khu. v, 41), sasurehi suṇitabbā hiṃsitabbā ti suṇisā (M. i, 427), dvinnaṃ janānaṃ kulasantānaṃ karotī ti vā suṇisā, kucchikabban ti karīsaṃ (Khu. i, 2), gabbhaṃ vimocetī ti suriso, tam andhakāravidhamanena sattānaṃ bhayaṃ surati hiṃsatī ti sūriyo[^49] (Vin. iii, 2; D. ii, 208, 255), rogaṃ hiṃsatī ti sirīso (D. ii, 3), ilati kampatī ti Illiso (Khu. v, 18), taṇhāya dubbalo hutvā ilati kampatī ti vā Illiso, pāpakaraṇe alati samatthetī ti alaso (Khu. v, 109), mahitabbo pūjetabbo ti mahiso, sīyati bandhīyatī ti sīsaṃ (M. i, 413), kitabbaṃ hiṃsitabban ti kisaṃ (Vin. i, 22\) icc evamādi.

There are ‘ussa’, ‘nusa’ and ‘isa’ after ‘manu’, ‘pūra’, ‘suṇa’ and others.

There are the suffixes ‘ussa’, ‘nusa’ and ‘isa’ after the stems and roots ‘manu’, ‘pūra’, ‘suṇa’, ‘ku’, ‘su’, ‘ila’, ‘ala’, ‘maha’, ‘si’, ‘ki’ and others. They are formed again.

Wholesome and unwholesome (kusalākusale) dhammas (dhamme) he knows (manati, jānāti), so (ti) human (mānuso); what is a cause and what is not a cause (kāraṇākāraṇaṃ) (manti jānāti), so (ti) or (vā) human (manusso, mānusso); what is beneficial and what is not beneficial (atthānatthaṃ) he knows (manati, jānāti), so (ti) or (vā) human (manusso, mānuso); of the mother and father (mātāpitūnaṃ) the heart (hadayaṃ) he fills (pūreti), so (ti) man (puriso); his own (attano) mind wish (manorathaṃ) he fills (pūreti), so (ti) or (vā) man (puriso), (pūreti), so (ti) or (vā) man (poso); by parents in law (sasurehi) she is to be heard (suṇitabbā, hiṃsitabbā), so (ti) daughter in law (suṇisā); of two peopel (dvinnaṃ janānaṃ) continuation of family (kulasantānaṃ) (karoti), so (ti) or (vā) daughter in law (suṇisā); it is to be disgusted (kucchikabban), so (ti) excrement (karīsaṃ); the fetus (gabbhaṃ) it releases (vimoceti), so (ti) name of plant that can cause abortion (suriso); that (tam) by destroying darkness (andhakāravidhamanena) of beings (sattānaṃ) fear (bhayaṃ) it hurts (surati, hiṃsati), so (ti) sun (sūriyo); disease (rogaṃ) it hurts (hiṃsati), so (ti) acacia tree (sirīso); he shakes (ilati, kampati), so (ti) Illiso; with attachment  (taṇhāya) weak (dubbalo) being (hutvā) he shakes (ilati, kampati), so (ti) or (vā) Illiso; in doing evil (pāpakaraṇe) he is able (alati, samattheti), so (ti) idle (alaso); it is to be honored (mahitabbo, pūjetabbo), so (ti) buffalo (mahiso); it is to be bound (sīyati, bandhīyati), so (ti) the head (sīsaṃ); it is to be hurt (kitabbaṃ, hiṃsitabban), so (ti) a thin person (kisaṃ), etc.
