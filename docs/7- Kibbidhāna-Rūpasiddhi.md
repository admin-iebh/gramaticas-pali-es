**7\-KIBBIDHĀNA-KAṆḌA**

**TEKĀLIKA**

**Kiccappaccayanta-naya**  
	  
Atha dhātūhi yeva bhāva-kamma-kattu-karaṇādisādhanasahitaṃ kibbidhānam ārabhīyate.

	Tattha kicca-kitakavasena duvidhā hi paccayā, tesu kiccasaññāya paṭhamaṃ vuttattā, kiccānam appakattā ca kiccappaccayā tāva vuccante.

	**Bhū** sattāyaṃ, “bhūyate, abhavittha, bhavissate vā Devadattenā” ti viggahe.

	“Dhātuyā kammādimhi ṇo”[^1] ti ito “dhātuyā” ti sabbattha paccayādividhāne vattate, “parā,” paccayā” ti ca adhikāro.

	**545\. Bhāva-kammesu tabbānīyā** (1125) \[V 27\].

	**Bhāva kamma** icc etesv atthesu sabbadhātūhi **tabba anīya** icc ete paccayā parā honti. Yogavibhāgena aññatthā pi.

	Tattha:

		Akammakehi dhātūhi, bhāve kiccā bhavanti te;  
		Sakammakehi kammatthe, araha-sakkatthadīpakā.

	Te ca:

	**546\. Ṇādayo tekālikā** (1137).

	Tikāle niyuttā **tekālikā**, ye idha tatiye dhātvādhikāre vihitā aniddiṭṭhakālā **ṇā**dayo paccayā, te tekālikā hontī ti paribhāsato kālattaye pi honti.

	Sīhagatiyā “kvacī” ti vattate.

	**547\. Yathāgamam ikāro** (210) \[V 170\].

	Yathāgamaṃ yathāpayogaṃ jinavacanānuparodhena dhātūhi paro **i**kārāgamo hoti kvaci byañjanādikesu **kicca-kittaka**ppaccayesu, “aññesu cā”[^2] ti vuddhi, “o ava sare”[^3] ti **avā**deso, “naye paraṃ yutte”[^4] ti paraṃ netabbaṃ.

	**548\. Te kiccā** (1131).

	Ye idha vuttā **tabbānīya-ṇya-teyya-ricca**ppaccayā, te **kicca**saññā hontī ti veditabbā. Tato “aññe kit”[^5] ti vacanato kiccappaccayānam akitakattā nāmabyapadese asampatte “taddhita-samāsa-kitakā nāmaṃ ‘v’ ātave-tunādīsu cā”[^6] ti ettha **ca**ggahaṇena nāmabyapadeso, tato **syā**dyuppatti. **Bhāve** bhāvass’ ekattā ekavacanam eva, “sin”[^7] ti **am**ādeso. Bhavitabbaṃ bhavatā paññena, bhavanīyaṃ.

	Idha byañjanādittābhāvā anuvattita-**kvaci**ggahaṇena **i**kārāgamābhāvo. **Bhāve** kiccappaccayantā napuṃsakā. **Kamme** tiliṅgā.

	**Kammani** abhipubbo, abhibhūyate, abhibhūyittha, abhibhūyissate ti abhibhavitabbo kodho paṇḍitena, abhibhavitabbā taṇhā, abhibhavitabbaṃ dukkhaṃ, evaṃ abhibhavanīyo, abhibhavanīyā, abhibhavanīyaṃ, **purisa-kaññā-citta**saddanayena netabbaṃ, evaṃ sabbattha.

	Ettha hi:

			Tabbādīh’ eva kammassa, vuttattā ‘va pun’ attanā;  
			Vattabbassa abhāvā na, dutiyā paṭhamā tato.

	**Āsa** upavesane, āsīyittha, āsīyate, āsīyissate ti āsitabbaṃ tayā, āsanīyaṃ. **Kamme** upāsitabbo garu, upāsanīyo.

	**Sī** saye, asīyittha, sīyate, sīyissate ti sayitabbaṃ bhavatā, sayanīyaṃ, “e ayā” ti **ayā**deso, atisayitabbo paro, atisayanīyo.

	**Pada** gatimhi, uppajjittha, uppajjate, uppajjissate ti uppajjitabbaṃ tena, uppajjanīyaṃ,[^8] ettha ca “kattarī” ti adhikāraṃ vinā “divādito yo”[^9] ti vinādhikārayogavibhāgena **ya**ppaccayo, “tassa cavagga-yakāra”[^10] icc ādinā **ca**vaggo, “paradvebhāvo ṭhāne”[^11] ti dvibhāvo. Paṭipajjitabbo maggo, paṭipajjanīyo.

	**Budha** avagamane, abujjhittha, bujjhate, bujjhissate ti bujjhitabbo dhammo, bujjhanīyo.

	**Su** savaṇe, asūyittha, sūyate, sūyissate ti sotabbbo dhammo, idha **yathāgama**ggahaṇena **i**kārāgamābhāvo. Suṇitabbo, “svādito ṇu-ṇa-uṇa cā”[^12] ti vinādhikārayogavibhāgena **ṇā**ppaccayo, savaṇīyo.[^13]

	**Kara** karaṇe, karīyattha, karīyati, karīyissatī ti atthe **tabbānīyā**.

	“Antassa,” “karassa,” “ca,” “tattan” ti ca vattate.

	**549\. Tuṃ-tuna-tabbesu vā** (1226). 

	**Kara** icc etassa dhātussa antabhūtassa **ra**karassa **ta**kārattaṃ hoti vā **tuṃ tuna tabba** icc etesu paccayesu paresu. Kattabbo bhavatā dhammo, kattabbā pūjā, kattabbaṃ kusalaṃ,[^14] **ta**ttābhāve “karotissā” ti vattamāne “tave-tunādīsu kā”[^15] ti ettha **ādi**saddena **tabbe** pi **kā**deso, kātabbaṃ hitaṃ.

	**550\. Ra-hādito ṇa** (1135) \[V 171\].

	**Ra**kāra-**ha**kārādyantehi dhātūhi parassa **anānīyā**di-**na**kārassa **ṇa**kāro hoti. **Ādi**saddena **ramu-apa-ñā-tā**dito pi.

		**Ra-hā**dito parass’ ettha, **na**kārassa asambhavā;  
		**Anānīyā**di **na**ss’ eva, sāmathyā ‘yaṃ **ṇa**kāratā.

	Karaṇīyo dhammo, karaṇāraho ti attho karaṇīyā, karaṇīyaṃ.

	**Bhara** bharaṇe, bharīyatī ti bharitabbo, bharaṇīyo.

	**Gaha** upādāne, agayhittha, gayhati, gayhissatī ti gahetabbo, “tesu vuddhī” ti ādinā **i**kārass’ **e**kāro,[^16] saṅgaṇhitabbo, “gahādito ppa-ṇhā”[^17] ti vinādhikārayogavibhāgena **ṇhā**paccayo**, ha**lopasaralopādi, saṅgaṇhaṇīyo,[^18] gahaṇīyo.

	**Ādi**ggahaṇena **ramu** kīlāyaṃ, ramīyittha, ramīyati, ramīyissatī ti ramitabbo, ramaṇīyo vihāro.

	**Apa** pāpuṇane, **uṇā**paccayo, pāpīyatī ti pāpuṇitabbo, “gupādīnañ cā”[^19] ti dhātvantassa lopo, dvittañ ca, pattabbo, patteyyo, pāpuṇaṇīyo,[^20] pāpaṇīyo.

	“Antassa,” “vā” ti ca vattate.

	**551\. Gama-khana-hanādīnaṃ tuṃ-tabbādīsu na** (1200) \[V 96\].

	**Gama khana hana** icc evamādīnaṃ **ma**kāra-**na**kārantānaṃ dhātūnam antassa **na**kāro hoti vā **tuṃ tabba tave tuna tvāna tvā** icc evamādīsu **t**akārādippaccayesu paresu. Agacchīyittha, gacchīyati, gacchīyissatī ti gantabbo maggo, gamitabbaṃ, gamanīyaṃ.

	**Khanu** avadāraṇe, akhaññittha, khaññati, khaññissatī ti khantabbaṃ āvāṭaṃ, khanitabbaṃ, “kvaci dhātū” ti ādinā **khana**ntassa **ṇa**ttañ ca, khaṇitabbaṃ, khaṇaṇīyaṃ, khananīyaṃ vā.

	**Hana** hiṃsā-gatīsu, ahaññittha, haññate, haññissate ti hantabbaṃ, hanitabbaṃ, hananīyaṃ.

	**Mana** ñāṇe, amaññittha, maññate, maññissate ti mantabbo, manitabbo, **ya**ppaccaye **ca**vaggādi, maññitabbaṃ, maññanīyaṃ.

	**Pūja** pūjāyaṃ, apūjīyittha, pūjīyati, pūjīyissatī ti atthe **tabbānīyā**. “Curādito ṇe-ṇayā”[^21] ti akattari pi **ṇe-ṇayā**, **i**kārāgam’-**ānīye**su “saralopo” ti ādinā kāritasarassa lopo, pūjetabbo, pūjayitabbo, pūjanīyo bhagavā.

	“Tabbānīyā” ti yogavibhāgena kattu-karaṇesu pi, **yā** pāpuṇane, niyyātī ti niyyānīko maggo.[^22] Gacchantī ti gamanīyā bhogā.[^23] **Naha** soce, nahāyati etenā ti nahānīyaṃ cuṇṇaṃ.[^24]

	“Bhāva-kammesū” ti adhikāro.

	**552\. Ṇyo ca** (1126) \[V 28\].

	Bhava-kammesu sabbadhātūhi **ṇya**ppaccayo hoti, **ca**ggahaṇena “ñāteyyan” ti ādīsu **teyya**ppaccayo ca.

	**553\. Kāritaṃ viya ṇānubandho** (1227).

	**Anubandho** appayogī, **ṇ**akārānubandho paccayo kāritaṃ viya daṭṭhabbo ti kāritabyapadeso, “kāritānaṃ ṇo lopan”[^25] ti **ṇ**alopo, “asaṃyogantassa vuddhi kārite”[^26] ti vuddhi, **i**kārāgamo, kattabbaṃ kāriyaṃ.

	**Hara** haraṇe, aharīyittha, harīyati, harīyissatī ti vā haritabbaṃ hāriyaṃ.

	**Bhara** bharaṇe, bharitabbaṃ bhāriyaṃ.

	**Labha** lābhe, labhitabbaṃ labbhaṃ, “yavataṃ ta-la-na”[^27] icc ādisutte **kāra**ggahaṇena yavato **bha**kārassa **bha**kāro, dvittaṃ.

	**Sāsa** anusiṭṭhimhi,[^28] sāsitabbo sisso, “kvaci dhātū” ti ādinā **ā**kārass’ **i**kāro.[^29]

	**Vaca** viyattiyaṃ  vācāyaṃ, **ṇya**ppaccayādimhi kate “antānaṃ,” “**ṇā**nubandhe” ti ca vattate.

	**554\. Ka-gā ca-jānaṃ** (1229) \[V 98\].

	**Ca ja** icc etesaṃ dhātvantānaṃ **ka**kāra-**ga**karādesā honti **ṇ**akārānubandhe paccaye pare  ti **ca**ssa **kā**deso. Vacanīyaṃ[^30] vākyaṃ.[^31]

	**Bhaja** sevāyaṃ, bhajanīyaṃ bhāgyaṃ,[^32] **ja**ssa **gā**deso.

	**Ci** caye, acīyittha, cīyati, cīyissatī ti cetabbaṃ ceyyaṃ, **i**kārass’ **e**kāro vuddhi, **ya**kārassa dvittaṃ, **vi-ni**pubbo “do dhassa cā”[^33] ti sutte **ca**ggahaṇena **ca**kārassa **cha**kāro,[^34] viniccheyyaṃ, vinicchitabbaṃ, vinicchanīyaṃ. “Kiyādito nā”[^35] ti vinādhikārayogavibhāgena **tabbānīya-tuṃ-tunā**dīsu ca **nā**paccayo, vinicchinitabbaṃ, vinicchinanīyaṃ.

	**Nī** pāpuṇane, anīyittha, nīyati, nīyissatī ti neyyo,[^36] neyyā, neyyaṃ. Netabbaṃ.

	**Ṇya**ggahaṇaṃ chaṭṭhīyantavasen’ ānuvattate, maṇḍūkagatiyā **anta**ggahaṇañ ca tatiyantavasena.

	**555\. Bhūto ‘bba** (1128) \[V 31\].

	**Bhū** icc etasmā parassa **ṇya**ppaccayassa saha dhātvantena **abbā**deso hoti. Bhavitabbo bhabbo,[^37] bhabbā, bhabbaṃ.

	“Ṇyassa,” “antenā” ti ca vattate.

	**556\. Vada-mada-gamu-yuja-garah’-ākārādīhi jja-mma-gga-yh’-eyyā gāro vā** (1129) \[V 30\].

	**Vada mada gamu yuja garaha** icc evamādīhi dhātūhi, **ā**kārantehi ca parassa **ṇya**ppaccayassa dhātvantena saha yathākkamaṃ **jja mma gga yha eyya** icc ete ādesā honti vā, **gara**ssa ca **gārā**deso, **garaha**ssa **gara**ss’ ev’ āyaṃ **gāro**. Vavatthitavibhāsattho ‘yaṃ **vā**saddo.

	**Vada** viyattiyaṃ vācāyaṃ, avajjittha, vajjati, vajjissatī ti vā vajjaṃ[^38] vadanīyaṃ, rassattaṃ. Vajjaṃ doso.

	**Mada** ummāde, amajjittha, majjate, majjissati etenā ti majjaṃ[^39] madanīyaṃ. **Mada**ggahaṇena karaṇe pi **ṇya**ppaccayo. **Gamu sappa** gatimhi, gantabbaṃ gammaṃ.

	**Yuja** yoge, ayujjittha, yujjate, yujjissatī ti yoggaṃ, niyojjo vā.

	**Garaha** nindāyaṃ, agarayhittha, garahīyati, garahīyissatī ti atthe **ṇya**ppaccayo, tass’ iminā dhātvantena saha **yhā**deso, **gara**ssa, **gārā**deso ca, gārayho,[^40] gārayhā, gārayhaṃ, garahaṇīyaṃ.

	**Ādi**saddena aññe pi **dama-jaha**ntā gayhante. **Gada** viyattiyaṃ vācāyaṃ. Gajjate, gadanīyaṃ vā gajjaṃ. **Pada** gatimhi, pajjanīyaṃ pajjaṃ gāthā. **Khāda** bhakkhaṇe, khajjate ti khajjaṃ[^41] khādanīyaṃ. **Damu** damane, adammittha, dammate, damīyissatī ti dammo damanīyo. **Bhuja** pālana-byavaharaṇesu, abhujjittha, bhujjati, bhujjissatī ti bhoggaṃ, bhojjaṃ[^42] vā, **kāra**ggahaṇena **ya**ssa **ja**kāro. Gahetabbaṃ gayham icc ādi.

	**Ā**kārantato pana **dā** dāne, adīyittha, dīyati, dīyissatī ti atthe **ṇya**ppaccayo, tass’ iminā dhātvantena **ā**kārena saha **eyyā**deso,[^43] deyyaṃ. Dātabbaṃ. **Anīye** “saralopo” ti ādinā pubbasarassa lope sampatte tatth’ eva **tu**ggahaṇena nisedhetvā “sarā sare lopan”[^44] ti **ā**kāre lutte parasarassa **dīgho**, dānīyaṃ.

	**Pā** pāne, apīyittha, pīyati, pīyissatī ti peyyaṃ, pātabbaṃ, pānīyaṃ. **Hā** cāge, ahīyittha, hīyati, hīyissatī ti heyyaṃ, hātabbaṃ, hānīyaṃ. **Mā** māne, amīyittha, mīyati, mīyissatī ti meyyaṃ, mātabbaṃ, minitabbaṃ, metabbaṃ vā. **Ñā** avabodhane, aññāyittha, ñāyati, ñāyissatī ti ñeyyaṃ, ñātabbaṃ, ñāteyyaṃ. “Ñāssa jā-jaṃ-nā”[^45] ti **jā**dese “kiyādito nā”[^46] ti vinādhikārayogavibhāgena **nā**paccayo, **i**kārāgamo ca, jānitabbaṃ, vijānanīyaṃ.[^47] **Khyā** pakathane, saṅkhyātabbaṃ, saṅkhyeyyaṃ icc ādi.

	**557\. Karamhā ricca** (1127) \[V 31\].

	**Kara**dhātuto **ricca**ppaccayo hoti bhāva-kammesu.

	**558\. Ramhi-r-anto rādi no** (1124) \[IV 132\].

	**R**akārānubandhe paccaye pare sabbo dhātvanto **r**ādi paccaya-**r**akāramariyādo no hoti, lopam āpajjate ti attho. **Ranto** ti ettha **r**akāro sandhijo, kattabbaṃ kiccaṃ.[^48] “Riccā” ti yogavibhāgena **bharā**dito pi **ricca**ppaccayo,[^49] yathā, bharīyatī ti bhacco, saralopo. **I** gatimhi, pati-etabbo paṭicco.

	**559\. Pesātisagga-pattakālesu kiccā** (1244) \[V 32\].

	**Pesa atisagga pattakāla** icc etesv atthesu **kicca**ppaccayā honti. **Pesanaṃ nāma** “kattabbam idaṃ bhavatā” ti āṇāpanaṃ, ajjhesanañ ca. **Atisaggo nāma** “kim idaṃ mayā katabban” ti puṭṭhassa vā “pāṇo na hontabbo” ti ādinā paṭipattidassanamukhena vā kattabbassa anuññā. **Pattakālo** nāma sampattasamayo yo attano kiccakaraṇasamayam anupaparikkhitvā na karoti, tassa samayārocanaṃ, na tattha ajjhesanam atthī ti. Te ca “bhāva-kammesu kicca-kta-kkhatthā”[^50] ti vuttattā bhāva-kammesv eva bhavanti.

	**Pesane** tāva: karīyatu bhavatā kamman ti atthe iminā **tabbānīyā**, sesaṃ vuttanayam eva, kattabbaṃ kammaṃ bhavatā, karaṇīyaṃ kiccaṃ bhavatā.

	**Atisagge** bhujjatu bhavatā ti atthe **tabbā**di, “aññesu cā” ti vuddhi.

	“Tassā” ti vattate.

	**560\. Bhujādīnam anto no dvi ca** (1181) \[V 95\].

	**Bhuja** icc evamādīnaṃ **ja**kārādi-antānaṃ dhātūnam anto no hoti, parassa kicca-kitaka-ppaccaya-**ta**kārassa ca dvebhāvo hoti. Bhottabbaṃ bhojanaṃ bhavatā, bhojanīyaṃ bhojjaṃ bhavatā.

	**I**kārāgamayutta-**ta**kāre pana: “na-ma-ka-rānam antānaṃ n’ iyuttatamhī”[^51] ti ettha “antānaṃ n’ iyuttatamhī” ti yogavibhāgena dhātvantalopādinisedho, “rudhādito niggahītapubban”[^52] ti vinādhikārayogavibhāgena, “niggahītañ cā”[^53] ti vā niggahītāgamo, bhuñjitabbaṃ tayā, yuñjitabbaṃ.

	**Samayārocane** pana: **i** ajjhayane **adhi**pubbo, adhīyataṃ bhavatā ti atthe **tabbānīyā**di, **i**kārāgama-vuddhi-**ayā**desa-**ajjhā**desā ca, **ṇya**ppaccaye tu vuddhi, **ya**kārassa dvittañ ca, ajjhayitabbaṃ, ajjheyyaṃ bhavatā, ajjhayanīyaṃ bhavatā, avassaṃ kattabban ti vākye pana “kiccā” ti adhikicca “avassakādhamiṇesu ṇī cā”[^54] ti avassakādhamiṇatthe ca **tabbā**dayo, kattabbaṃ me bhavatā gehaṃ, karaṇīyaṃ, kāriyaṃ. Evaṃ dātabbaṃ me bhavatā sataṃ, dānīyaṃ, deyyaṃ.

	**Dhara** dhāraṇe, curādittā **ṇe-ṇayā**, vuddhi, **i**kārāgamo ca, dhāretabbaṃ, dhārayitabbaṃ icc ādi.

	“Nudādīhi yu-ṇvūnam an’-ānanāk’-ānanakā sakāritehi cā”[^55] ti sakāritehi pi **yu-ṇvū**nam ādesavidhānato yeva dhātuppaccayantato pi kicca-kitakappaccayā bhavantī ti daṭṭhabbo. Tena titikkhāpīyatī ti titikkhāpetabbo. Evaṃ tikicchāpetabbo tikicchāpanīyo. Abhāvīyittha, bhāvīyati, bhāvīyissatī ti bhāvetabbo maggo. Bhāvayitabbo, bhāvanīyo, bhāvanīyaṃ, bhāvanīyā, akārīyittha, kārīyati, kārīyissatī ti kāretabbaṃ, kārayitabbaṃ, kārāpetabbaṃ, kārāpayitabbaṃ, kārāpanīyam icc ādi ca siddhaṃ bhavati.

			Kattabbaṃ karaṇīyañ ca, kāriyaṃ kiccam icc pi;  
			Kāretabbaṃ tathā kārā-petabbaṃ kiccasaṅgaho.

Kiccappaccayanta-nayo.

**Tekālika**

**Kitakappaccayanta-naya.**

	Idāni kitakappaccayā vuccante.

	**Kara** karaṇe, pure viya dhātusaññādi.

	Kumbha icc upapadaṃ, tato dutiyā.

	“Kumbhaṃ karoti, akāsi, karissatī” ti vā viggahe:

	“Parā,” “paccayā” ti ca vattate.

	**561\. Dhātuyā kammādimhi ṇo** (1106) \[V 41\].

	Kammasmiṃ ādimhi sati dhātuyā paro **ṇa**ppaccayo hoti.

	So ca:

	**562\. Aññe kit** (1132).

	Tatiye dhātvādhikāre vihitā kiccehi aññe paccayā **kit** icc eva saññā hontī ti **kit**saññā kātā.

	**563\. Kattari kit** (1231).

	Kattari kārake **kit**paccayo hotī ti niyamato kattari bhavati, so ca “ṇādayo tekālikā”[^56] ti vuttattā kālattaye ca hoti. Pure viya kāritabyapadesa-**ṇ**alopavuddhiyo, paccayantass’ āliṅgattā **syā**dimhi asampatte “taddhita-samāsa-kitakā nāmaṃ ‘v’ ātave-tunādīsu cā”[^57] ti kitakantattā nāmaṃ ‘va kate **syā**dyuppatti, tato kumbhaṃ karotī ti atthe “amādayo parapadebhī”[^58] ti dutiyātappurisasamāso, “nāmānan”[^59] ti ādinā samāsasaññā, “tesaṃ vibhattiyo lopā cā”[^60] ti vibhattilopo, “pakati c’ assa sarantassā”[^61] ti pakatibhāvo, puna samāsattā nāmam iva kate **syā**dyuppatti.

	So kumbhakāro,[^62] te kumbhakārā icc ādi. Itthiyaṃ kumbhakārī, kumbhakāriyo icc ādi, tathā kammaṃ karotī ti kammakāro. Evaṃ mālākāro,[^63] kaṭṭhakāro, rathakāro, suvaṇṇakāro, suttakāro, vuttikāro, ṭīkākāro.

	**Gaha** upādāne, pattaṃ agaṇhi, gaṇhāti, gaṇhissatī ti vā pattaggāho. Evaṃ rasmiggāho,[^64] rajjuggāho.

	**Ve** tantasantāne,[^65] tantaṃ avāyi, vāyati, vāyissatī ti vā tantavāyo,[^66] “te āv’-āyā kārite”[^67] ti **āyā**deso, vākye pan’ ettha “te āv’-āyā” ti yogavibhāgena **āyā**deso. Evaṃ tunnavāyo.

	**Mā** parimāṇe, dhaññaṃ amini, mināti, minissatī ti vā atthe **ṇa**ppaccaye kate.

	“Ṇamhī” ti vattate.

	**564\. Ākārantānam āyo** (1197) \[V 91\].

	**Ā**kārantānaṃ dhātūnaṃ antassa **āyā**deso hoti **ṇ**akārānubandhe paccaye pare, saralopādi. Dhaññamāyo. Evaṃ dānaṃ dadātī ti dānadāyo.

	**Kamu** kantimhi, dhammaṃ akāmayi, kāmayati, kāmayissatī ti vā dhammakāmo[^68]  puriso, dhammakāmā kaññā, dhammakāmaṃ cittaṃ. Evaṃ atthakāmo, hitakāmo,[^69] sukhakāmo,[^70] dhammaṃ pāletī ti dhammapālo[^71] icc ādi.

	**Damu** damane, “ariṃ adami, dameti, damissatī” ti viggahe “dhātuyā” ti adhikāro, “kammādimhī” ti ca vattate.

	**565\. Saññāyam a nu** (1107).

	Kammūpapade ādimhi sati saññāyaṃ gamyamānāyaṃ dhātuyā **a**ppaccayo hoti, upapadante **nu**kārāgamo ca. Ettha ca “nu niggahītaṃ  padante”[^72] ti sutte “padante” ti vacanato upapadante yeva **nu**kārāgamo hotī ti daṭṭhabbaṃ. “Tesu vuddhī” ti ādinā **u**kāralopo. Ayaṃ pana **nvā**gamo samāsaṃ katvā upapadavibhattilope kate yeva hotī ti veditabbaṃ.

	**566\. Nu niggahītaṃ padante** (1122).

	Upapadabhūtanāmapadante vattamāno **nu**kārāgamo **niggahītam** āpajjate, niggahītassa vaggantattaṃ, sesaṃ samaṃ, vuddhābhāvo ‘va viseso, Arindamo[^73] rājā.

	Tathā **tara** taraṇe, vessaṃ taratī ti Vessantaro,[^74] taṇhaṃ karoti hiṃsatī ti Taṇhaṅkaro bhagavā. Evaṃ Medhaṅkaro, Saraṇaṅkaro, Dīpaṅkaro.

	“Ādimhi,” “a” iti ca vattate.

	**567\. Pure dadā ca iṃ** (1108).

	**Pura**sadde ādimhi sati “**dada** dāne” icc etāya dhātuyā **a**ppaccayo hoti, **pura**sadde **a**kārassa **iñ** ca hoti. Ettha ca “tadanuparodhenā”[^75] ti paribhāsato **pura**saddantass’ eva **iṃ** hotī ti daṭṭhabbaṃ. **Ṇā**dīnaṃ tekālikatte pi upapadatthavisesena atīte yev’ āyam **a**ppaccayo hotī ti daṭṭhabbaṃ. Pure dānaṃ adadī ti Purindado[^76] Sakko. Idhā pi vibhattilope kate yeva **iṃ**ādeso.

	“Kammādimhi,” “a” iti ca vattate.

	**568\. Sabbato ṇvu-tv-āvī vā** (1109) \[V 33-4, 44\].

	Sabbato dhātuto kammādimhi vā akammādimhi vā sati **a ṇvu tu āvī** icc ete cattāro paccayā honti. **Vā**ggahaṇaṃ “akammādimhi vā” ti vikappanatthaṃ.

	**A**ppaccaye tāva: **dhara** dhāraṇe, dhammaṃ adhari, dharati, dharissatī ti vā dhammadharo.[^77] Evaṃ vinayadharo.[^78] Tathā taṃ karotī ti takkaro, dvittaṃ. Evaṃ hitakaro, divasakaro, dinakaro, divākaro, nisākaro, dhanuṃ gaṇhātī ti dhanuggaho.[^79] Evaṃ kaṭaggaho,[^80] sabbakāmaṃ dadātī ti sabbakāmadado, sabbadado.[^81]

	**Āto** pana: annaṃ adāsi, dadāti, dadissatī ti annado.[^82] Evaṃ dhanado, saccaṃ sandahatī ti saccasandho.[^83] **Pā** pāne, majjaṃ pivatī ti majjapo. **Tā** pālane, gavaṃ saddaṃ tāyatī ti gottaṃ.[^84] Evaṃ kattari.

	Akammādimhi pana “yasmād apetī”[^85] ti sutte **bhaya**ggahaṇena sesasādhane pi **a**ppaccayo.

	**Nī** pāpuṇane **vi**pubbo, vinesi, vineti, vinessati etena, etthā ti vā vinayo,[^86] “aññesu cā”[^87] ti vuddhi, **ayā**deso ca, nayanaṃ nayo. **Si** sevāyaṃ **ni**pubbo, nissīyittha, nissīyati, nissīyissatī ti vā nissayo.[^88] **Si** saye, anusayi, anuseti, anusessatī ti vā anusayo.[^89]

	**I** gatimhi **pati**pubbo, paṭicca etasmā phalam etī ti paccayo,[^90] samudayo.[^91] **Ci** caye, vinicchīyate anena, vinicchayanaṃ vā vinicchayo,[^92] uccayanaṃ uccayo, sañcayo, dhammaṃ vicinātī ti dhammavicayo. **Khī** khaye, khayanaṃ khayo.[^93] **Ji** jaye, vijayanaṃ vijayo, jayo. **Kī** dabbavinimaye, vikkayanaṃ vikkayo,[^94] kayo. **Lī** silesane, allīyati etthā ti ālayo,[^95] layo. Evaṃ **i**vaṇṇantato.

	Āsuṇantī ti assavā, avādeso, paṭissavanaṃ paṭissavo. **Su** gatimhi, ābhavaggā savantī ti āsavā.[^96] **Ru** sadde, ravatī ti ravo. Bhavatī ti bhavo. Pabhavati etasmā ti pabhavo.[^97] **Lū** chedane, lavanaṃ lavo. Evaṃ **u**vaṇṇanatato.

	Niggaṇhāti, niggahaṇaṃ vā niggaho, paggaho, saṅgaṇhāti tena, saṅgahaṇaṃ vā saṅgaho.[^98] **Vara** varaṇe, saṃvaraṇaṃ saṃvaro. **Dara** ādare, ādaraṇaṃ ādaro. Āgacchati, āgamanan ti vā āgamo,[^99] āgamīyanti ettha, etena vā atthā ti āgamo pariyatti. Sappatī ti sappo. Dibbatī ti devo. **Kamu** padavikkepe, pakkamanaṃ, pakkamatī ti vā pakkamo. Evaṃ vikkamo.

	**Cara** caraṇe, vane caratī ti vanacaro, kāmo avacarati etthā ti kāmāvacaro loko, kāmāvacarā saññā, kāmāvacaraṃ[^100] cittaṃ. Gāvo caranti etthā ti gocaro,[^101] chaṭṭhītappuriso.

	Pādena pivatī ti pādapo.[^102] Evaṃ kacchapo,[^103] tatiyātappuriso.

	**Ruha** janane, sirasmiṃ ruhatī ti siroruho, guhāyaṃ sayatī ti guhāsayaṃ[^104] cittaṃ. Evaṃ kucchisayā vātā.[^105] **Ṭhā** gatinivattimhi, pabbate aṭṭhāsi, tiṭṭhati, ṭhassatī ti vā pabbataṭṭho puriso, pabbataṭṭhā nadī, pabbataṭṭhaṃ osadhaṃ. Evaṃ thalaṭṭhaṃ, jalaṭṭhaṃ, sattamītappuriso.

	**569\. Gahass’ upadhass’ e vā** (1238).

	**Gaha** icc etassa dhātussa upadhassa **e**ttaṃ hoti vā, **upadhā** ti antakkharato pubbakkharasa parasamaññā,[^106] gayhatī ti gehaṃ, gahaṃ vā.

	**Ṇvu**ppaccaye rathaṃ karotī ti atthe **ṇvu**ppaccayo, so ca “aññe kit”[^107] ti **kit**saññattā “kattari kit”[^108] ti kattari yeva bhavati, tato kāritabyapadesa-**ṇ**alopa-vuddhiyo.

	**570\. An’-akā yu-ṇvūnaṃ** (1228).  
	  
	**Yu ṇvu** icc etesaṃ paccayānaṃ **ana aka** icc ete ādesā  hontī ti **akā**deso. Sesaṃ kumbhakārasaddasamaṃ, rathakārako. Tathā annaṃ dadātī ti annadāyako,[^109] “ākārantānam āyo”[^110] ti **āyā**deso. “Itthiyam ato āpaccayo”[^111] ti **ā**paccayo, “tesu vuddhī” ti ādinā **a**kārassa **i**kāro,[^112] annadāyikā kaññā, annadāyakaṃ kulaṃ. Lokaṃ netī ti lokanāyako,[^113] vineti satte ti vināyako,[^114] “te āv’-āyā kārite”[^115] ti **āyā**deso.

	**Akammūpapade** karotī ti kārako, kārikā, kārakaṃ. Dadātī ti dāyako,[^116] dāyikā, dāyakaṃ. Netī ti nāyako, nāyikā, nāyakaṃ. Bhagavato ovādānusāsaniṃ asuṇi, suṇāti, suṇissatī ti vā savako,[^117] sāvikā, **āvā**deso. Lunātī ti lāvako. **Pu** pavane, punātī ti pāvako, bhavatī ti bhāvako, upāsatī ti upāsako,[^118] upāsikā. Gaṇhātī ti gāhako.

	Pacatī ti pācako. Ayaji, yajati, yajissatī ti vā yājako. Ettha hi “ka-gā ca-jānan”[^119] ti **ca-jānaṃ** **ka-ga**tte sampatte.

	**571\. Na ka-gattaṃ ca-jā ṇvumhi** (1224).

	Dhātvantabhūtā **ca**kāra-**ja**kārā **ka**kāra-**ga**kārattaṃ n’ āpajjante **ṇvu**ppaccaye pare ti paṭisiddhattā na bhavati.

	**Jana** janane, janetī ti janako, janikā, “ghaṭādīnaṃ vā”[^120] ti ettha **vā**ggahaṇena vuddhi na hoti. Evaṃ khanatī ti khanako, sametī ti samako, gametī ti gamako, dametī ti damako, ahani, hanti, hanissantī ti vā vadhako, “vadho vā sabbatthā”[^121] ti **hana**ssa **vadhā**deso. Hantī ti ghātako, “hanassa ghāto”[^122] ti **ṇvu**mhi **ghātā**deso, gāvo hanatī ti goghātako,[^123] rundhatī ti rundhako, niggahītāgamo, saṃyogantattā na vuddhi hoti.[^124] Evaṃ bhuñjatī ti bhuñjako, kiṇātī ti kāyako, pāletī ti pālako, pūjetī ti pūjako.

	**572\. Nudādīhi yu-ṇvūnam an’-ānanāk’-ānanakā sakāritehi ca** (1268).

	**Nudā**dīhi dhatūhi, sakāritehi ca dhātūhi paresaṃ **yu-ṇvu**ppaccayānaṃ yathākkamaṃ **ana ānana aka ānanaka** icc ete ādesā honti.

	Ettha hi:

			Sakāritehi **yu**\-**ṇvū**naṃ, kāriyassa vidhānato;  
			Kicca-kitsambhavo dhātu-ppaccayehi pi vediyo.

	**Nuda** khepe **pa**pubbo, panudi, panudati, panudissatī ti vā atthe **ṇvu**ppaccayo, tass’ iminā **akā**deso, “kvaci dhātū” ti ādinā **nudi**ssa dīgho,[^125] panūdako.

	**Sūda** paggharaṇe, sūdatī ti sūdako. **Ñā** avabodhane, aññāsi, jānāti, jānissatī ti vā atthe **ṇvu**ppaccayo, tass’ ānena **ānanakā**deso, “ñāssa jā-jaṃ-nā”[^126] ti **jā**deso, saralopādi, jānanako.

	**Sakāritehi** pana **aṇa** pesane, āṇāpesi, āṇāpeti, āṇāpessatī ti atthe “sabbato ṇvu-tv-āvī vā”[^127] ti **ṇvu**ppaccayo, tass’ iminā **akā**deso, saralopādi, āṇāpako, saññāpetī ti saññāpako, sañjānanako, ettha **ānanakā**deso, “kvaci dhatū” ti ādinā kāritalopo. Tathā dāpetī ti dāpako, “an’-akā yu-ṇvūnan”[^128] ti **akā**deso, patiṭṭhāpetī ti patiṭṭhāpako, nibbānaṃ sampāpetī ti nibbānasampāpako, kārāpetī ti kārāpako, kārāpikā icc ādi.

	**Tu**ppaccaye akāsi, karoti, karissatī ti vā atthe “sabbato ṇvu-tv-āvī vā”[^129] ti **tu**ppaccayo, so ca **kit**saññattā **nvu**ppaccayo viya sabbattha kattari yeva bhavati. 

	“Antassā” ti vattate.

	**573\. Karassa ca tattaṃ tusmiṃ** (1225).

	**Kara** icc etassa dhātussa antassa **ra**kārassa **ta**kārattaṃ hoti **tu**ppaccaye pare. **Ca**saddena **bharā**dīnañ ca, tato nāmam iva kate **syā**dyuppatti, “satthu-pitādīnam ā sismiṃ silopo cā”[^130] ti **ā**ttaṃ, **si**lopo, tassa kattā takkattā, chaṭṭhīsamāso. Tathā bharatī ti bhattā.

	**Hara** haraṇe, haratī ti hattā, bhindatī ti bhettā, bheditā vā, chindatī ti chettā, dadātī ti dātā, bhojanassa dātā bhojanadātā, sandahatī ti sandhātā, avaci, vacati, vakkhatī ti vā vattā, “bhujādīnam anto no dvi cā”[^131] ti dhātvantalopo, dvittañ ca, bhuñjatī ti bhottā, abujjhi, bujjhati, bhujjhissatī ti vā bujjhitā, **ya**kār’-**i**kārāgamā, jānātī ti ñātā, jinātī ti jetā, suṇātī ti sotā, gaṇhātī ti gahetā, bhavatī ti bhavitā, saratī ti saritā, gacchatī ti gantā. “Gama-khana-hanādīnaṃ tuṃ-tabbādīsu na”[^132] iti dhātvantassa **na**ttaṃ. Evaṃ khanatī ti khantā, hanatī ti hantā, maññatī ti mantā, pāletī ti pāletā, pālayitā.

	**Kārite** bhāvetī ti bhāvetā, bhāvayitā. Evaṃ sāretā, sārayitā, dāpetā, dāpayitā; hāpetā, hāpayitā, nirodhetā, nirodhayitā, bodhetā, bodhayitā, ñāpetā, ñāpayitā, sāvetā, sāvayitā; gāhetā, gāhayitā; kāretā, kārayitā, kārāpetā, kārāpayitā icc ādi.

	**Āvī**paccaye **disa** pekkhane, bhayaṃ apassi, passati, passissatī ti vā atthe **avī**paccayo, “kvaci dhātū” ti ādinā **disa**ssa **dassā**deso, bhayadassāvī, bhayadassāvino icc ādi **daṇḍī** ‘va neyyaṃ. **Itthiyaṃ** bhayadassāvinī. **Napuṃsake** bhayadassāvi cittaṃ.

	**Sāsa** anusiṭṭhimhi, sadevakaṃ lokaṃ diṭṭhadhammikādivasena sāsatī ti atthe.

	**574\. Sāsādīhi ratthu** (1159).

	**Sāsa** icc evamādīhi dhātūhi **ratthu**ppaccayo hoti. “Ramhi-r-anto rādi no”[^133] ti **rā**dilopo, saralopādi, nāmabyapadeso, **syā**dyuppatti, **ā**ttaṃ, **si**lopo. Satthā,[^134] satthāro.

	**Pā** rakkhaṇe, puttaṃ pātī ti atthe.

	**575\. Pādito ritu** (1160).

	**Pā** icc evamādito dhātuggaṇato **ritu**ppaccayo hoti, **rā**dilopo saralopādi. Pitā.[^135] **Dhara** dhāraṇe, mātāpitūhi dharīyatī ti dhītā, “kvaci dhātū” ti ādina **i**kārassa dīgho.

	**Māna** pūjāyaṃ, dhammena puttaṃ mānetī ti atthe.

	**576\. Mānādīhi rātu** (1161).

	**Māna bhāsa** icc evamādīhi dhātūhi **rātu**ppaccayo hoti, **rā**dilopo, mātā.[^136] **Bhāsa** viyattiyaṃ vācāyaṃ, pubbe bhāsatī ti bhātā icc ādi.

	**Visa** pavesane **pa**pubbo, pāvisi, pavisati, pavisissatī ti vā atthe.

	**577\. Visa-ruja-padādito ṇa** (1110) \[V 44\].

	**Visa ruja pada** icc evamādīhi dhātūhi paro **ṇa**ppacayo hotī ti **ṇa**ppaccayo. So ca **kit**saññattā kattari bhavati, kāritabyapadesa-**ṇa**lopa-vuddhiyo, paveso.

	Tathā **ruja** roge, aruji, rujati, rujissatī ti vā rogo,[^137] “ka-gā ca-jānan”[^138] ti **ja**kārassa **ga**kāro, uppajjatī ti uppādo. **Phusa** phusane, aphusi, phusati, phusissati, phusanti vā tena sampayuttā ti phasso,[^139] “kvaci dhātū” ti ādinā **phusa**ssa **phasso**, saṃyogantattā na vuddhi. Bhavatī ti bhāvo. **Uca** samavāye, ucatī ti oko, **ca**kārassa **ka**kāro. **Aya** gatimhi, ayi, ayati, ayissati, ayati vā ito ti āyo. **Budha** avagamane, sammā bujjhatī ti sambodho, āharatī ti āhāro,[^140] upahanatī ti upaghāto, “hanassa ghāto”[^141] ti **ghātā**deso.

	**Ranja** rāge, ranjatī ti atthe **ṇa**ppaccayo.

	**578\. Niggahīta[^142] saṃyogādi no** (1212).

	Saṃyogasmiṃ ādibhūto **na**kāro niggahītam āpajjate. Niggahītassa vaggantattaṃ, **ja**kārassa **ga**ttaṃ, raṅgo.

	**579\. Ṇamhi ranjassa jo bhāva-karaṇesu** (1194).

	**Ranja** icc etassa dhātussa antabhūtassa **nja**ssa **ja**kārādeso hoti bhāva karaṇa icc etesv atthesu vihite **ṇa**kāravati ppaccaye pare.

	Ettha hi:

			**Ṇa**mhi **ranja**ssa karaṇe, **jā**desassa vidhānato;  
			Akattari pi viññeyyo, kārake **ṇa**ssa sambhavo ti.

	Ranjanti anenā ti rāgo, rañjīyati anenā ti vā rāgo,[^143] sayaṃ rañjatī ti pi rāgo. “Ṇamhi ranjassa jo” ti yogavibhāgena **ja**kāro. Pajjate anenā ti pādo, patujjate anenā ti patodo, jarīyati anenā ti jāro.[^144] Evaṃ dāro. Tathā **kammādīsu**, bhujjatī ti bhogo.[^145] Evaṃ bhāgo, bhāro, labbhatī ti lābho,[^146] Voharīyatī ti vohāro,[^147] dīyatī ti dāyo,[^148] vihaññati etasmā ti vighāto, viharanti etthā ti vihāro,[^149] āramanti etasmin ti ārāmo.[^150] Evaṃ papāto icc ādi.

	“Ṇa” iti vattate.

	**580\. Bhāve ca** (1111) \[V 44\].

	Bhāvatthe bhāvābhidheyye[^151] dhātūhi **ṇa**ppaccayo hoti. Bhūyate, bhavanaṃ vā bhāvo. Paccate, pacanaṃ vā pāko, “ka-gā ca-jānan” ti **kā**deso.

	**Sica** paggharaṇe, secanaṃ seko. **Suca** soke, socananṃ soko.[^152] **Caja** hānimhi, acajjittha, cajjate, cajjissate, cajanaṃ vā cāgo.[^153] **Yaja** devapūjā-saṅgatikaraṇa-dānesu, ijjittha, ijjate, ijjissate, yajanaṃ vā yāgo, yuñjananṃ yogo. **Bhaja** sevāyaṃ, abhajjittha, bhajjate, bhajjissate, bhajanaṃ vā bhāgo, arajjittha, rajjate, rajjissate, rajanaṃ vā rāgo, **ja**ssa **ga**kāro.

	**Daha** bhasmīkaraṇe, pariḍayhittha, pariḍayhati, pariḍayhissati, pariḍayhanaṃ vā ti atthe **ṇa**ppaccayo.

	“Ṇamhi,” “vā” ti ca vattate.

	**581\. Dahassa do laṃ** (1219) \[V 127\].

	**Daha** icc etassa dhātussa **da**kāro **la**ttam āpajjate **ṇa**ppaccaye pare vā. Pariḷāho, paridāho. **Bhanja** avamaddane, bhañjanaṃ bhaṅgo. **Sanja** saṅge, sañjanaṃ saṅgo,[^154] **na**ssa niggahītaṃ.

	Paccayehi saṅgamma karīyati, saṅkharīyati tena vā ti atthe **visa-ruja-padā**dinā, saṅkharaṇan ti atthe “bhāve cā”[^155] ti vā **ṇa**ppaccayo.

	“Ṇamhī” ti vattate.

	**582\. Pura-sam-upa-parīhi karotissa kha-kharā vā tappaccayesu ca** (1198) \[V 133-4\].

	**Pura saṃ upa pari** icc etehi parassa **karoti**ssa dhātussa **kha khara** icc ete ādesā honti vā **ta**ppaccaye, **ṇa**ppaccaye ca pare. “Tappaccayesū” ti bahuvacananiddesena **tuṃ-tvā**dīsu pi. Dhātvādesass’ āpi ṭhānopacārena dhātuvohārato “asaṃyogantassa vuddhi kārite”[^156] ti vuddhi, saṅkhāro.[^157] Evaṃ parikkhāro,[^158] purekkhāro.

	**Vā** ti kiṃ? Upakāro.

	**Lubha** giddhimhi, lubbhanti tena, sayaṃ vā lubbhati, lubbhanamattam eva vā tan ti lobho.[^159] **Dusa** appītimhi, dussanti tena, sayaṃ vā dussati, dussanamattam eva tan ti doso.[^160] **Muha** vecitte, muyhanti tena, sayaṃ vā muyhati, muyhanamattam eva tan ti moho[^161] icc ādi kattu-karaṇa-bhāvesu yathārahaṃ yojetabbaṃ.

	**Gaha** upādāne, gayhatī ti atthe **visa-ruja-padā**dinā kammani **ṇa**ppaccayo.

	**583\. Gahassa ghara ṇe vā** (1218).

	**Gaha** icc etassa dhātussa **gharā**deso hoti vā **ṇa**ppaccaye pare, saralopādi, gharaṃ, gharāni.

	**Vā** ti kiṃ? Gaṇhāti, gahaṇaṃ vā gāho.

	Sambhavatī ti atthe.

	**584\. Kvi ca** (1112) \[V 47\].

	Sabbadhūhi **kvi**paccayo hoti, so ca **kit**saññattā kattari bhavati.

	**585\. Kvilopo ca** (1266) \[V 159\].

	**Kvi**no sabbassa lopo hoti. Kitantattā nāmam iva katvā **syā**dyuppatti, **si**lopo, sambhū. Evaṃ vibhavatī ti vibhū, abhibhū, sayambhū.

	Tathā **dhū** kampane, sandhunātī ti sandhū. **Bhā** dittimhi, vibhātī ti vibhā, pabhātī ti pabhā, saha, saṅgamma vā bhanti, bhāsanti vā etthā ti sabhā,[^162] **saha**ssa **sā**deso, niggahītalopo ca.

	Bhujena gacchatī ti atthe **kvi**ppaccayo.

	**586\. Dhātvantassa lopo kvimhi** (1220) \[V 94\].

	Dhātvantassa byañjanassa lopo hoti **kvi**ppaccaye pare. Kvilopo, bhujago.[^163] Evaṃ urasā gacchatī ti urago, turaṃ sīghaṃ turitaturito gacchatī ti turago, khe[^164] gacchatī ti khago, vihāyase[^165] gacchatī ti vihago, **vihā**deso, na gacchatī ago, nago.[^166]

	**Khanu** avadāraṇe **saṃ**pubbo, saṅkhani, saṅkhanati, saṅkhanissatī ti vā saṅkho. **Ramu** kīḷāyaṃ, kuñje ramatī ti kuñjaro. **Jana** janane, kammato jāto ti atthe **kvi**ppaccayo, dhātvantassa lopādi purimasamaṃ, pañcamītappuriso ‘va viseso. Kammajo vipāko, kammajā paṭisandhi, kammajaṃ[^167] rupaṃ. Evaṃ cittajaṃ, utujaṃ, āhārajaṃ, attajo putto. Vārimhi jāto vārijo.[^168] Evaṃ thalajo, paṅkajaṃ, jalajaṃ, aṇḍajaṃ, sirajaṃ,[^169] sattamīsamāso. Dvikkhattuṃ jāto dvijo, pacchā jāto anujo icc ādi.

	**Vida** ñāṇe, lokaṃ avedī ti atthe **kvi**ppaccayo.

	“Kvimhī” ti vattate

	**587\. Vidante ū** (1222) \[V 38\].

	**Vida**dhātuno ante **ū**kārāgamo hoti **kvi**mhi, kvilopo. Lokavidū.[^170]

	**Disa** pekkhaṇe, imam iva  naṃ apassi, passati, passissatī ti, ayam iva dissatī ti vā atthe **kvi**ppaccayo.

	“Dhātvantassa lopo kvimhī” ti dhātvantalope sampatte.

	**588\. I-ya-ta-ma-ki-e-sānam antassaro dīghaṃ kvaci disassa guṇaṃ, do raṃ, sa-kkh’ ī ca** (1269) \[III 85-7, 90; V 43, 125\]. 

	**Ima ya ta amha kiṃ eta samāna** icc etesaṃ sabbanāmānaṃ upamānupapadabhāvena **disa**ssa dhātussa guṇabhūtānaṃ anto saro **dīgham** āpajjate, **disa** icc etassa dhātussa antassa **sa kkha ī** icc ete ādesā ca honti. **Disa**ssa **da**kāro **ra**kāram āpajjate ti kvimhi dhātvantassa **sa**saddādesaṃ katvā kvilopādimhi ca kate **i** iti nipātanena **ima**saddass’ **i**kāre, tass’ iminā dīghe ca kate **syā**dyuppatti.

	Īdiso[^171] puriso, īdisā kaññā, īdisī vā, īdisaṃ cittaṃ. Tathā yam iva naṃ passati, yo viya dissatī ti vā yādiso,[^172] yādisā, yādisī, yādisaṃ. Tam iva naṃ passati, so viya dissatī ti vā tādiso,[^173] tādisā, tādisī, tādisaṃ. Mam iva naṃ passati, ahaṃ viya so dissatī ti vā  mādiso,[^174] mādisā, mādisī, mādisaṃ; **ma** iti nipātanena **amha**saddassa **ma**saddādeso. Kim iva naṃ passati, ko viya dissatī ti vā kīdiso, kīdisā, kīdisī, kīdisaṃ. Etam iva naṃ passati, eso viya dissatī ti vā ediso, etādiso vā, edisā, edisī, edisaṃ; **e** iti nipātanena **eta**saddassa **e**kāro. Samānaṃ katvā naṃ  passati, samāno viya dissatī ti sādiso, sadiso; **sa** iti nipātanena **samāna**ssa **sā**deso, tadantassa vā dīgho, sādisā, sādisī, sadisā, sadisī, sādisaṃ, sadisaṃ.

	**Da**kārassa **ra**kārādese pana īriso, yāriso, tāriso, māriso, kīriso, eriso, sāriso, sariso. **Kkhā**dese idikkho, yādikkho, tādikkho, mādikkho, kīdikkho, edikkho, sādikkho, sadikkho, **ra**kārādese sārikkho, sarikkho; **ī**kārādese īdī, yādī, tādī,[^175] mādī, kīdī, edī, sādī.

	**Ca**saddena **tumhā**di-upapade pi tumhe viya dissatī ti tumhādiso, tumhādisī,[^176] khandhā viya dissantī ti khandhādisā icc ādi.

	**Dhara** dhāraṇe, apāyesv apatamāne adhigatamaggādike satte dhāreti, dharanti tenā ti vā, salakkhaṇaṃ dhāreti vā, paccayehi dharīyati vā ti atthe.

	589\. **Dharādīhi rammo** (1113).

	**Dhara** icc evamādīhi dhātūhi **ramma**ppaccayo hoti.

	So ca:

			**Kamma**ggahaṇato **bhāva-kammesū** t’ ettha vediyo;  
			Akattari pi hotī ti, kārake **ramma**ppaccayo.

	**Rā**dilopo, dhammo,[^177] evaṃ karīyatī ti kammaṃ. **Vara** varaṇe, vammaṃ.

	**Saṃsa** pasaṃsane **pa**pubbo, piya icc upapadaṃ, piyaṃ pasaṃsituṃ sīlaṃ yassā ti vā piyaṃ pasaṃsanasīlo, piyaṃ  pasaṃsanadhammo, piyaṃ pasaṃsane sādhukārī ti vā atthe.

	**590\. Tassīlādīsu ṇī-tv-āvī ca** (1114) \[V 53\].

	**Sīlaṃ** pakati, tassīla-taddhamma-tassādhukārīsv atthesu gamyamānesu sabbadhātūhi **ṇī tu āvī** icc ete paccayā hontī ti kattari **ṇī**paccayo, saṃyogantattā na vuddhi. Sesaṃ neyyaṃ.

	Piyapasaṃsī rājā. Atha vā piyaṃ pasaṃsi, pasaṃsati, pasaṃsissati vā sīlena vā  dhammena vā sādhu vā ti piyapasaṃsī, piyapasaṃsinī, piyapasaṃsi kulaṃ. Brahmaṃ carituṃ sīlaṃ yassā ti vā brahmaṃ carati sīlena, dhammena, sādhu vā ti brahmacārī,[^178] brahmacārinī, brahmacāri. Evaṃ saccavādī,[^179] dhammavādī,[^180] sīghayāyī, pāpakārī,[^181] mālākārī icc ādi.

	**Ca**saddena attamāne pi **ṇī**,[^182]  paṇḍitaṃ attānaṃ maññatī ti paṇḍitamānī[^183] bālo, bahussutamānī icc ādi.

	**Vatu** vattane **pa**pubbo, pasayha pavattituṃ sīlaṃ yassā ti atthe iminā **tu**ppaccayo, pasayhapavattā. Atha vā **vaca** viyattiyaṃ vācāyaṃ, pasayha pavattituṃ sīlam assā ti pasayhapavattā, pasayhapavattāro, **bhujā**dittā dhātvantalopa-dvittāni, sesaṃ **kattu**samaṃ.

	Bhayaṃ passitūṃ sīlaṃ yassā ti vā bhayaṃ dassanasīlo, bhayaṃ dassanadhammo, bhayaṃ dassane sādhukārī ti vā bhayadassāvī,[^184] bhayadassāvinī, bhayadassāvi cittaṃ. Evaṃ ādīnavadassāvī.

	“Tasīlādīsū” ti adhikāro.

	**591\. Sadda-kudha-cala-maṇḍattha-rucādīhi yu** (1117).

	**Sadda-kudha-cala-maṇḍa**tthehi dhātūhi, **rucā**dīhi ca **yu**ppaccayo hoti tassīlādīsv atthesu.

	**Ghusa** sadde, ghosituṃ sīlaṃ assā ti ghosanasīlo ti vā aghosayi, ghosayati, ghosayissati sīlena, dhammena, sādhu vā ti atthe iminā **yu**ppaccayo, tassa “an’-akā yu-ṇvūnan”[^185] ti **anā**deso, “aññesu cā ti”[^186] vuddhi, so ghosano, sā ghosanā. **Bhāsa** viyattiyaṃ vācāyaṃ, bhāsituṃ sīlam assā ti vā bhāsanasīlo, bhāsanadhammo, bhāsane sādhukārī ti vā bhāsano.

	**Kudha** kope, kujjhituṃ sīlam assā ti vā kujjhanasīlo ti vā kodhano, kodhanā, kodhanaṃ.

	**Rusa** rose, rosituṃ sīlam assā ti vā rosanasīlo ti vā rosano.

	**Cala** kampane, calituṃ sīlaṃ yassā ti vā calati sīlenā ti vā calano. **Kapi** calane, kampituṃ sīlaṃ yassā ti vā akampi, kampati, kampissati sīlenā ti vā kampano, **i**kārānubandhidhātusarato “kvacī dhātū” ti ādinā, “niggahītañ cā” ti vā niggahītāgamo. **Phadi** kiñcicalane, phandituṃ sīlaṃ yassā ti vā phandati sīlenā ti vā phandano.

	**Maḍi** bhūsāyaṃ, maṇḍayituṃ sīlaṃ yassā ti vā maṇḍayati sīlenā ti vā maṇḍano. **Bhūsa** alaṅkāre, bhūsanasīlo ti vā abhūsayi, bhūsayati, bhūsayissati sīlenā ti vā bhūsano, bhūsanā, bhūsanaṃ.

	**Ruca** dittimhi, arucci, ruccati, ruccissati sīlenā ti vā rocano. **Juta** dittimhi, ajoti, jotati, jotissati sīlenā ti vā jotano. **Vaḍḍha** vaḍḍhane, vaḍḍhituṃ sīlam assā ti vaḍḍhano icc ādi.

	**592\. Pārādigamimhā rū** (1118) \[V 42\].

	**Pārā**di-upapadehi parasmā **gami** icc etasmā dhātumhā paro **rū**paccayo hoti tassīlādīsv atthesu kattari yeva. Pāro ādi yesaṃ te pārādayo, pārādīhi gami **pārādigami**. **Rā**dilopo, bhavapāraṃ gantuṃ sīlaṃ yassā ti vā bhavapāraṃ gamanasīlo, bhavapāraṃ gamanadhammo, bhavapāraṃ gamane sādhukārī ti vā bhavapāragū, bhavapāraguno. Antaṃ gamanasīlo antagū. Evaṃ vedagū,[^187] addhagū.

	“Rū” ti vattate.

	**593\. Bhikkhādito ca** (1119).

	**Bhikkha** icc evamādīhi dhātūhi **rū**paccayo hoti tassīlādīsv atthesu. **Bhikkha** yācane, bhikkhituṃ sīlaṃ yassā ti vā abhikkhi, bhikkhati, bhikkhissati sīlenā ti vā bhikkhanadhammo ti vā bhikkhane sādhukārī ti vā bhikkhu,[^188] “kvaci dhātū” ti ādinā rassattaṃ. **Ikkha** dassan’-aṅkesu, saṃsāre bhayaṃ ikkhatī ti pi bhikkhu, vijānituṃ sīlaṃ yassa, vijānanasīlo ti vā viññū,[^189] sabbaṃ jānātī ti sabbaññū.[^190] Evaṃ mattaññū,[^191] dhammaññū,[^192] atthaññū,[^193] kālaññū,[^194] kataññū[^195] icc ādayo.[^196]

	**594\. Hanatyādīnaṃ ṇuko** (1120).

	**Hanatyā**dīnaṃ dhātūnam ante **ṇuka**ppaccayo hoti tassīlādīsv atthesu kattari, antāpekkhāyaṃ chaṭṭhī, **ṇ**akāro vuddhattho. Āhananasīlo āghātuko, ghātādeso, saralopādi, karaṇasīlo kāruko[^197] sippī. **Bhī** bhaye bhāyanasīlo bhīruko, **r**akārāgamo. **Ava** rakkhaṇe, āvuko pitā.

	**595\. Saṃhan’-aññāya vā ro gho** (1123).

	**Saṃ**pubbāya **hana** icc etāya dhātuyā, aññāya ca dhātuyā paro **ra**ppaccayo hoti, **hana**ssa **gho** ca. **Vā**ggahaṇaṃ sampiṇḍanatthaṃ, vikappanatthaṃ vā, tena saṅghāto ti pi siddhaṃ hoti.

		**Hana**ss’ ev’ āyaṃ **gho** hoti, abhidhānānurūpato;  
			Asaṃpubbā ca **r**o tena, paṭigho ti pi sijjhanti.[^198]

	**Hana** hiṃsā-gatīsu **saṃ**pubbo, saṃhanati samaggaṃ kammaṃ samupagacchati, samma-d-eva kilesadarathe hanatī ti vā saṃgho,[^199] **rā**dilopo, samantato nagarassa bāhiye khaññatī ti parikhā, itthiyaṃ **ā**paccayo, antaṃ karotī ti antako maccu.

	“Bhāva-kammesū” ti vattate.

	**596\. Nandādīhi yu** (1133) \[V 48\].

	**Nanda** icc evamādīhi dhātūhi paro **yu**ppaccayo hoti bhāva-kammesu. “An’-akā yu-ṇvūnan”[^200] ti **yu**ppaccayassa **anā**deso, **nanda** samiddhimhi, **nanda** nandane vā. **Bhāve:** nandīyate, nandanaṃ. **Kamme:** anandīyittha, nandīyati, nandīyissati, nanditabban ti vā nandanaṃ vanaṃ, gayhati, gahaṇīyaṃ vā gahaṇaṃ, gaṇhanaṃ vā, caritabbaṃ caraṇaṃ, bhūyate bhavanaṃ, hūyate havanaṃ. Rundhitabbaṃ rundhanaṃ, rodhanaṃ vā bhuñjitabbaṃ bhuñjanaṃ, bhojanaṃ vā. Bujjhitabbaṃ bujjhanaṃ, bodhanaṃ vā. Sūyati, suti vā savaṇaṃ, pāpīyatī ti pāpuṇanaṃ, pāpanaṃ vā, pālīyatī ti pālanaṃ icc ādi.

	“Yū” ti vattate.

	**597\. Kattu-karaṇa-padesesu ca** (1134) \[V 48\]. 

	**Kattu karaṇa padesa** icc etesv atthesu ca sabbadhātūhi **yu**ppaccayo hoti. Ettha ca **padeso** ti adhikaraṇakārakaṃ vuccati. **Kattari** tāva: rajaṃ haratī ti rajoharaṇaṃ[^201] toyaṃ. Āramaṇaṃ vijānātī ti viññāṇaṃ,[^202] vijānanaṃ vā, **ānana-jā**desā. **Ghā** gandhopādāne, ghāyatī ti ghānaṃ,[^203] **jhe** cintāyaṃ, jhāyatī ti jhānaṃ,[^204] “kvaci dhatū” ti ādinā **ā**ttaṃ.

	**Karaṇe: kara** karaṇe, karoti tenā ti karaṇaṃ, yathāsarūpaṃ saddā byākarīyanti etenā ti byākaraṇaṃ.[^205] **Pūra** pūraṇe, pūrayati tenā ti pūraṇaṃ.[^206] Dīyati anenā ti dānaṃ,[^207] pamīyati anenā ti pamānaṃ,[^208] vuccati anenā ti vacanaṃ,[^209] panudati,  panujjate anenā ti vā panūdano. **Sūda** paggharaṇe, sūdati sujjate anenā ti vā sūdano, suṇāti, sūyati etenā ti vā savaṇaṃ.[^210] **Lū** chedane, lunāti, lūyati anenā ti vā lavanaṃ, lavaṇaṃ, loṇaṃ[^211] vā. Nayati, nīyati etenā ti vā nayanaṃ. **Pū** pavane, punāti, pūyate anenā ti vā pavano, sameti, samīyati vā pāpaṃ anenā ti samaṇo,[^212] samaṇaṃ vā. Tathā bhāveti, bhāvīyati etāyā ti vā bhāvanā.[^213] Evaṃ pācanaṃ, pācāpanaṃ icc ādi.

	**Adhikaraṇe: ṭhā** gatinivattimhi, tiṭṭhati tasmin ti ṭhānaṃ.[^214] Evaṃ sayanaṃ,[^215] senaṃ vā, āsanaṃ,[^216] adhikarīyati etthā ti adhikaraṇaṃ.

	**Ca**saddena **sampadānāpādāne**su pi sammā pakārena dadāti assā ti sampadānaṃ, apecca etasmā ādadātī ti apādānaṃ.

	**598\. Saññāyaṃ dā-dhāto i** (1138) \[V 45\].

	Saññāyaṃ gamyamānāyaṃ **dā dhā** icc etehi dhātūhi **i**ppaccayo hoti, bhāva-kammādi-adhikār’ ev’ āyaṃ, saralopādi. **Dā** dāne **ā**pubbo, ādīyatī ti ādi.[^217] Evaṃ upādi.[^218] **Dhā** dhāraṇe, udakaṃ dadhātī ti udadhi,[^219] **tesu vuddhi-lopā**dinā saññāyaṃ **udaka**ssa **udā**deso.[^220] Jalaṃ dhīyate asmin ti jaladhi, vālāni dadhāti tasmin ti vāladhi,[^221] sandhīyati, sandadhātī ti vā sandhi,[^222] nidhīyatī ti nidhi.[^223] Evaṃ vidhīyati, vidadhāti, vidhānaṃ vā vidhi,[^224] sammā, samaṃ vā cittaṃ ādadhātī ti samādhi.[^225]

	**599\. Itthiyam a-ti-yavo vā** (1140) \[V 49\].

	Itthiyaṃ abhidheyyāyaṃ sabbadhātūhi **a**kāra **ti yu** icc ete paccayā honti vā bhāva-kammādīsu. **A**ppaccaye tāva **jara** vayohānimhi, jīrati, jīraṇan ti vā jarā,[^226] “itthiyam ato āpaccayo”[^227] ti **ā**paccayo, paṭisambhijjatī ti paṭisambhidā.[^228] Paṭipajjati etāyā ti paṭipadā.[^229] Evaṃ sampadā,[^230] āpadā.[^231] Upādīyatī ti upādā.[^232] Sañjānātī ti saññā,[^233] pajānātī ti paññā.[^234] Upekkhatī ti upekkhā.[^235] Cintanaṃ cintā.[^236] Patiṭṭhānaṃ patiṭṭhā.[^237] **Sikkha** vijjopādāne, sikkhanaṃ, sikkhīyatī ti vā sikkhā.[^238] Evaṃ bhikkhā.[^239] **Jhe** cintāyaṃ, parasampattiṃ abhimukhaṃ jhāyatī ti abhijjhā,[^240] hitesitaṃ upaṭṭhapetvā jhāyatī ti upajjhā,[^241] upajjhāyo,[^242] sammā jhāyati etthā ti sajjhā.

	**Isu** icchāyaṃ, esanan ti atthe **a**ppaccayo, “isu-yamūnam anto ccho vā”[^243] ti **cchā**deso, icchā.[^244] **Puccha** pucchane, pucchanaṃ pucchā, tikicchanaṃ tikicchā, ghasitum icchā jighacchā, titikkhā,[^245] bubhukkhā, pātum icchā pipāsā,[^246] maṇḍūkagatiyā **vā**dhikārato **vā**desābhāvo. Byāpitum icchā vicchā icc ādi.

	**Ti**ppaccaye sambhavanaṃ sambhūti. **Vā**dhikārato **ti**ppaccayamhi na vuddhi, savaṇaṃ suti, nayanaṃ, nīyati etāyā ti vā nīti. **Mana** ñāṇe, maññatī ti mati.

	“Te,” “no,” “timhī” ti ca vattate.

	**600\. Gama-khana-hana-ramādīnam anto** (1190) \[V 109\].

	**Gama khana hana rama** icc evamādīnaṃ **ma**kāra-**na**kārantānaṃ dhātūnaṃ anto byañjano no hoti **ta**ppaccaye, **ti**mhi cā ti dhātvantalopo. Gamanaṃ, gantabbā ti vā gati,[^247] upahananaṃ  upahati, ramanti tāya, ramaṇaṃ vā rati.[^248] **Tanu** vitthāre, tananaṃ tati. **Yamu** uparame, niyamanaṃ niyati. “Ramate, ramatī” ti ādīsu pana **a**kārabyavahitattā na dhātvantalopo, bhuñjanaṃ bhutti, yuñjanaṃ yutti, “bhujādīnam anto no dvi cā”[^249] ti dhātvantalopo, dvittañ ca. Samāpajjanaṃ, samāpajjate ti vā samāpatti,[^250]  sampatti,[^251] “gupādīnañ cā”[^252] ti dhātvantalopa-dvittāni. “Kvaci dhātū” ti ādinā **hā**dito **ti**ssa **ni** hoti.[^253] Hāni, jāni icc ādi.

	**Yu**ppaccaye **cita** sañcetane, cetayatī ti atthe **yu**ppaccayo, **anā**desa-vuddhī, **ā**paccayo, cetanā.[^254] **Vida** anubhavane, vedayatī ti vedanā.[^255] **Disī** uccāraṇe, desīyatī ti desanā,[^256] bhāvīyatī ti bhāvanā icc ādi.

	“Itthiyaṃ,” “vā” ti ca vattate.

	**601\. Karato ririya** (1141) \[III 51\].

	**Kara**dhātuto itthiyam anitthiyaṃ vā abhidheyyāyaṃ **ririya**ppaccayo hoti, **rā**dilopo. Kattabbā kiriyā. Karaṇīyaṃ kiriyaṃ.[^257]

	“Kattarī” ti vattate.

	**602\. Jito ina sabbattha** (1145).

	**Ji** icc etāya dhātuyā paro **ina**ppaccayo hoti sabbakāle kattari. **Ji** jaye, pāpake akusale dhamme ajini, jināti, jinissatī ti vā jino.[^258]

	“Inā” ti vattate.

	**603\. Supato ca** (1146).

	**Supa** icc etāya dhātuyā ca paro **ina**ppaccayo hoti. **Supa** saye, supati, supanan ti vā supino,[^259] supinaṃ.

	**Sī** saye, “īsaṃ” iti upapadaṃ, īsaṃ sīyati bhavatā ti atthe.

	**604\. Īsaṃ-du-sūhi[^260] kha** (1147).[^261]

	**Īsaṃ du su** icc etehi upapadehi parehi dhātūhi **kha**ppaccayo hoti.

	So ca.

	**605\. Bhāva-kammesu kicca-kta-kkhatthā** (1232).

	**Bhāva kamma** icc etesv atthesu **kicca-kta-kkha**ttha icc ete paccayā hontī ti niyamato bhāva-kammesv eva hoti. “Kvaci dhātū” ti **kha**kārānubandhassa lopo, vuddhi, **ayā**desa-dvittāni, īsassayo bhavatā, dukkhena sīyati dussayo, sukhena sīyati sussayo.

	**Kamme:** īsaṃ karīyatī ti īsakkaraṃ kammaṃ bhavatā.[^262] Evaṃ dukkhena karīyatī ti dukkaraṃ[^263] hitaṃ bhavatā, sukaraṃ[^264]  pāpaṃ bālena, dukkhena bharīyatī ti dubbharo[^265] mahiccho. Sukhena bharīyatī ti subharo[^266] appiccho. Dukkhena rakkhitabban ti durakkhaṃ[^267] cittaṃ. Dukkhena passitabbo ti duddaso[^268] dhammo. Sukhena passitabban ti sudassaṃ[^269] paravajjaṃ. Dukkhena anubujjhitabbo ti duranubodho[^270] dhammo. Sukhena bujjhitabban ti subodham icc ādi.

	**Budha** avagamane, sabbe saṅkhatāsaṅkhata-sammutibhede dhamme abujjhi, bujjhati, bujjhissatī ti vā atthe.

	“Ta” iti vattate.

	**606\. Budha-gamāditthe kattari** (1144).

	**Budha gamu** icc evamādīhi dhātūhi tadatthe gamyamāne kattari **ta**ppaccayo hoti sabbakāle.

	“Tassā” ti vattate.

	**607\. Dha-ḍha-bha-hehi dha-ḍhā ca** (1179) \[V 145-6\].

	**Dha-ḍha**nta-**bha-ha**ntehi dhātūhi parassa paccaya-**ta**kārassa yathākkamaṃ **dha**kāra-**ḍha**kārādesā hontī ti **dha-bha**to **ta**kārassa **dha**kāro, “ha-catutthānan”[^271] ti ettha **ha**kāraggahaṇato **ha**kārato pi kvaci **dha**ttaṃ, abyavadhāne c’ āyaṃ, tena “rundhati, ārādhito, vaḍḍhito, labhitvā, gahito” ti ādīsu paccayāgamabyavahitattā na bhavati.

	**608\. Ha-catutthānam antānaṃ do dhe** (1216).

	**Ha**kāra-vaggacatutthānaṃ dhātvantabhūtānaṃ **da**kārādeso hoti **dha**kāre pare. Buddho[^272] bhagavā. Saraṇaṃ agacchi, gacchati, gacchissatī ti vā saraṇaṅgato upāsako, “gama-khana-hana-ramādīnam anto”[^273] ti dhātvantalopo. Evaṃ jānātī ti ñāto. **I** gatimhi, upetī ti upeto. **Cinta** cintāyaṃ, cintetī ti cittaṃ,[^274] “gupādīnañ cā”[^275] ti dhātvantalopa-dvittāni. **Sanja** saṅge, rūpādīsu asajji, sajjati, sajjissatī ti vā satto,[^276] “bhujādīnam anto no dvi cā”[^277] ti dhātvantalopo, dvittañ ca.

	“Saññāyam” iti vattate.

	**609\. Ti kic c’ āsiṭṭhe** (1139).

	Saññāyam abhidheyyāyaṃ āsitthe gamyamāne dhātūhi **ti**ppaccayo hoti, **kit**paccayo ca. Jino enaṃ bujjhatū ti Jinabuddhi, **dha**kāra-**da**kārādesā, dhanam assa bhavatū ti Dhanabhūti.

	**Kit**paccaye bhavatū ti Bhūto, dhammo enaṃ dadātū ti Dhammadinno, “bhidādito inna-anna-īṇā vā”[^278] ti **ta**ppaccayassa **innā**deso. Vaḍḍhatū ti Vaḍḍhamāno, “bhūvādito a”[^279] iti **mān’-ante**su **a**ppaccayo, nandatū ti Nandako,[^280] jīvatū ti Jīvako[^281] icc ādi.[^282]

	**610\. Āgamā tuko** (1162).

	**Ā**pubbo **gami**to **tuka**ppaccayo hoti, kitakattā kattari. Āgacchatī ti āgantuko.[^283]

	“Gamā” ti vattate.

	**611\. Bhabbe ika** (1163).

	**Gami**to **ika**ppaccayo hoti bhabbatthe. Gantuṃ bhabbo ti gamiko bhikkhu.

Tekālikappaccayanta-nayo.

**Atītappaccayanta-naya**

	**612\. Atīte ta-tavantu-tāvī** (1142) \[V 55\].

	Atīte kāle sabbehi dhātūhi **ta tavantu tāvī** icc ete paccayā honti. Ete eva parasamaññāya **niṭṭha**saññakā pi, te ca **kit**saññattā kattari bhavanti. Abhavī ti bhūto,[^284] bhūtā, bhūtaṃ, “aññesu cā” ti etth’ ānuvattita-**vā**ggahaṇena **ta-tavantu-tāvī**su vuddhi na hoti. **Hu** dān’-ādana-habyappadānesu, ahavī ti huto[^285] aggiṃ.

	**Tavantu**ppaccaye: “Ā simhī”[^286] ti **ā**kāro, aggiṃ hutavā, hutavanto icc ādi guṇavantusamaṃ. **Tāvī**mhi: aggiṃ hutāvī, aggiṃ hutāvino icc ādi daṇḍīsamaṃ. Itthiyaṃ **inī**paccayo: hutāvinī, napuṃsake: rassattaṃ hutāvi.

	**Vasa** nivāse, vassaṃ avasī ti atthe **ta**ppaccayo, **sa**kārantattā “sādi santa”[^287] icc ādinā **ṭhā**dese sampatte.

	“Tassā” ti adhikāro, “sādī” ti ca.

	**613\. Vasato uttha** (1177).

	**Vasa** icc etasmā dhātumhā parassa **ta**kārassa sahādibyañjanena **utthā**deso hoti, saralopādi, vassaṃ vuttho,[^288] vutthā sā, “saralopo” ti ādīsutte **tu**ggahaṇato pubbalopābhāve “adhivatthā[^289] devatā, vatthabban” ti ādīsu paralopo.

	“Vasassā” ti vipariṇāmena vattate.

	**614\. Vassa vā-v-u** (1178) \[V 110-11\].

	**Vasa** icc etassa dhātussa **va**kārassa **ta**kāre pare **u**kāro hoti, tattha **va**kārāgamo ca vā hoti. Niṭṭha-**ta**kāre ev’ āyaṃ. Atha vā “vū” ti ettha **va**kāro sandhijo, tantañāyena dutiyañ c’ ettha **va**ggahaṇam icchitabbaṃ, tena **a**kārassa pi **u**kāro siddho bhavati, usito brahmacariyaṃ, vusito, tathā vusitavā, vusitāvī, **i**kārāgamena byavahitattā **utthā**deso na bhavati.

	**Bhuja** pālana-byavaharaṇesu, odanaṃ abhuñjī ti atthe **ta-tavantu-tāvī**, “bhujādīnam anto no dvi cā”[^290] ti dhātvantalopo, **ta**kārassa dvittañ ca, bhutto, bhuttavā, bhuttāvī. Tathā **ranja** rāge, arañjī ti ratto,[^291] rattā, rattaṃ. **Yuja** yoge, ayuñjī ti yutto,[^292] yuttā, yuttaṃ. **Vica** vivecane **vi**pubbo, viviccī ti vivitto,[^293] vivittā, vivittaṃ. **Muca** mocane, amuccī ti bandhanā mutto.[^294] Tathā **ti**ppaccaye pi iminā dhātvantalopa-dvittāni, āsajjanaṃ āsatti, vimuccanaṃ, vimuccati etāyā ti vā vimutti.[^295]

	**Kudha** kope, akujjhī ti atthe **ta**ppaccayo, tassa “dha-ḍha-bha-hehi dha-ḍhā cā”[^296] ti **dha**ttaṃ, “ha-catutthānam antānaṃ do dhe”[^297] ti **dha**kārassa **da**kāro, kuddho.[^298] **Yudha** sampahāre, ayujjhī ti yuddho,[^299] yuddhaṃ. **Sidha** saṃsiddhimhi, asijjhī ti siddho. **Ā**pubbo **rabha** rābhasse, ārabhī ti āraddho[^300] gantuṃ. **Naha** bandhane **saṃ**pubbo, sannayhī ti sannaddho,[^301] “dha-ḍha-bha-hehi dha-ḍhā cā” ti **nahā**dito **ta**kārassa **dha**kāro.

	**Vaḍḍha** vaḍḍhane, avaḍḍhī ti atthe **ta**ppaccayo, **ta**ssa **ḍha**ttaṃ, “kvaci dhātū” ti ādinā dhātvākārass’ **u**ttaṃ, **ḍa**lopo ca.

	“Hacatutthānam antānan” ti vattate.

	**615\. Ḍo ḍhakāre** (1217).

	Ha-catutthānaṃ dhātvantānaṃ **ḍa**kārādeso hoti **ḍha**kāre pare. Vuḍḍho,[^302] vuḍḍhā,[^303] “bo vassā” ti **ba**tte buḍḍho, **Ti**ppaccaye: bujjhanaṃ, bujjhati vā etāyā ti buddhi. Evaṃ siddhi, vaḍḍhi.[^304] **Tabba**ppaccaye: boddhabbam icc ādi.

	“Anto,” “no” ti ca adhikāro.

	**616\. Tarādīhi iṇṇo** (1184) \[V 152-3\].

	**Tara** icc evamādīhi dhātūhi parassa **ta**ppaccayassa **iṇṇā**deso hoti, dhātvanto ca no hoti, saralopādi. **Tara** taraṇe, saṃsāraṇṇavaṃ atarī ti tiṇṇo tāreyyaṃ.[^305] Evaṃ uttiṇṇo, tiṇṇaṃ vā. **Pūra** pūraṇe, saṃpūrī ti saṃpuṇṇo, “saralopo”[^306] ti ādisutte **tu**ggahaṇato pubbalopābhāve **u**vaṇṇato parassa “vā paro asarūpā”[^307] ti lopo, saṃyoge rassattaṃ. **Tura** vege, aturī ti tuṇṇaṃ, turitaṃ vā. **Jara** vayohānimhi, parijīrī ti parijiṇṇo.[^308] **Kira** vikiraṇe, ākirī ti ākiṇṇo[^309]

	**617\. Susa-paca-sakato kkha-kkā ca** (1186) \[V 155-6\].

	**Susa paca saka** icc etehi dhātūhi parassa **ta**ppaccayassa **kkha-kkā**desā honti, anto ca byañjano no hoti. **Ca**saddena **mucā**dito **kkā**deso.[^310] **Susa** sosane, asussī ti sukkho[^311] rukkho. Apaccī ti pakkaṃ[^312] phalaṃ. **Saka** sāmatthe, asakkhī ti sakko[^313] assa, omuccī ti omukkā[^314] upāhanā. “Pacituṃ, pacitabban” ti ādīsu pana na bhavati, **i**kārena byavahitattā. Evaṃ sabbattha byavadhāne na bhavati.

	Sīhagatiyā **ti**ggahaṇam anuvattate.

	**618\. Pakkamādīhi nto ca** (1187).

	**Pakkama** icc evamādīhi **ma**kārantehi dhātūhi parassa **ta**ppaccayassa **ntā**deso hoti, dhātvanto ca no hoti. **Ca**saddena **ti**ppaccayassa **nti** ca hoti.[^315]  **Kamu** padavikkhepe, pakkamī ti pakkanto.[^316] Evaṃ saṅkanto,[^317] nikkhanto,[^318] “do dhassa cā”[^319] ti sutte **ca**ggahaṇena **ka**ssa **kha**ttaṃ. **Bhamu** anavaṭṭhāne, vibbhamī ti vibbhanto,[^320] bhanto. **Khamu** sahane, akkhamī ti khanto. **Samu** upasame, asamī ti santo.[^321] **Damu** damane, adamī ti danto.[^322]

	**Ti**mhi: saṅkamanaṃ saṅkanti. Evaṃ okkanti, vibbhanti, khanti, santi, danti icc ādi.

	**619\. Janādīnam ā timhi ca** (1189) \[V 116\].

	**Jana** icc evamādīnaṃ dhātūnam antassa byañjanassa **ā**ttaṃ hoti **ta**ppaccaye, **ti**mhi ca. Yogavibhāgena aññatthā pi. **Jana** janane, ajanī ti jāto,[^323] vijāyīti puttaṃ vijātā, jananaṃ jāti.[^324] **Ta**ppaccaye sati pi **ta**kare puna **ti**ggahaṇakaraṇaṃ paccayantara**\-ta**kāre **ā**ttanivattanatthaṃ, yathā: jantu. “Janitvā, janitun” ti ādīsu pana **i**kārena byavahitattā na bhavati.

	“Ā,” “timhi,” “cā” ti ca vattate.

	**620\. Ṭhā-pānam i-ī ca** (1192) \[V 114-5\].

	**Thā pā** icc etesaṃ dhātūnaṃ antassa **ā**kārassa yathākkamaṃ **i**kāra-**ī**kārādesā honti **ta**ppaccaye, **ti**mhi ca. **Ca**saddena aññatrā pi kvaci. **Ṭhā** gatinivattimhi, aṭṭhāsī ti ṭhito, upaṭṭhito garuṃ, ṭhitavā, adhiṭṭhitvā, ṭhānaṃ thiti. **Pā** pāne, apāyī ti pītā, yāguṃ pītavā, pānaṃ pīti, pītvā.

	**621\. Hantehi ho hassa lo vā adaha-nahānaṃ** (1193) \[V 106-7\].

	**Ha**kārantehi dhātūhi parassa **ta**ppaccayassa, **ti**ssa ca **ha**kārādeso hoti, **ha**ssa ca dhātvantassa **la**kāro hoti vā **daha-nahe** vajjetvā, **ḍha**ttāpavādo ‘yaṃ. **Ruha** janane, āruhī ti āruḷho[^325] rukkhaṃ. **La-ḷā**nam aviseso, ārulho vā, ruhanaṃ ruḷhī. **Gahu** viloḷane, agāhī ti gāḷho, ajjhogāḷho mahaṇṇavaṃ.[^326] **Baha** vuddhimhi, abahī ti bāḷho, “kvaci dhātū” ti ādinā dīgho.[^327] **Muha** vecitte, amuyhī ti mūḷho.[^328] **Guha** saṃvaraṇe, aguhī ti gūḷhaṃ.[^329] **Vaha** pāpuṇane, upavahī ti upavuḷho,[^330] “vaca-vasa-vahādīnam ukāro vassā”[^331] ti yogavibhāgena **u**ttaṃ.

	**Adaha-nahānan** ti kimatthaṃ? Daḍḍho, sannaddho. **Vā** ti kiṃ? Duddho, siniddho. “Gahitaṃ, mahitan” ti ādīsu pana **i**kārāgamena byavahitattā na bhavati.

	Dhātuppaccayantato pi “atīte ta-tavantu-tāvī”[^332] ti **ta**ppaccayo, abubhukkhī ti bubhukkhito. Evaṃ jighacchito, pipāsito icc ādi.

Evaṃ kattari niṭṭhanayo.

	“Atīte” ti vattate.

	**622\. Bhāva-kammesu ta** (1143) \[V 56\].

	Atīte kāle gamyamāne sabbadhātūhi **ta**ppaccayo hoti **bhāva kamma** icc etesv atthesu.

	**Bhāve:** tāva **ge** sadde, gāyanaṃ, agāyitthā ti vā atthe **ta**ppaccayo.

	**623\. Sabbattha ge gī** (1165).

	**Ge** icc etassa dhātussa **gī**\-ādeso hoti sabbattha. **Ta**ppaccaya-**ti**paccayesv ev’ āyaṃ. Tassa gītaṃ,[^333] gāyanaṃ, gāyitabbā ti vā gīti.

	Bhāve **ta**ppaccayantā napuṃsakā, kammani tiliṅgā.

	**Nata** gattavināme, naccanaṃ, anaccitthā ti vā atthe **ta**ppaccayo.

	**624\. Paccayā-d-aniṭṭhā nipātanā sijjhanti** (1164).

	Ye idha sappaccayā saddā paccayehi na nitthaṃ gatā, te nipātanato sijjhantī ti dhātvantena saha **ta**ppaccayassa **cca-ṭṭā**desā.[^334] Niccaṃ,[^335] naṭṭaṃ. **Hasa** hasane, hasanaṃ hasitaṃ,[^336] **i**kārāgamo. Gamanaṃ gataṃ. Evaṃ ṭhitaṃ, sayitaṃ, **vā**dhikārassa vavatthitavibhāsattā vuddhi. **Ruda** assuvimocane, arujjhitthā ti roditaṃ, ruṇṇaṃ vā icc ādi.

	**Kammani:** abhibhūyitthā ti abhibhūto kodho bhavatā, abhibhūtā, abhibhūtaṃ. **Bhāsa** byattiyaṃ vācāyaṃ, abhāsittha tenā ti bhāsito[^337] dhammo, bhāsitā gāthā, bhāsitaṃ suttaṃ. **Disī** uccāraṇe, curādittā **ṇe**. Adesīyitthā ti desito[^338] dhammo bhagavatā, **i**kārāgame kāritasaralopo. **Ji** jaye, ajīyitthā ti jito[^339] māro. **Nī** pāpuṇane, anīyiṃsū ti nītā gāmam ajā, suto tayā dhammo, ñāto.

	**Sāsa** anusiṭṭhimhi, anusāsīyitthā ti atthe **ta**ppaccayo.

	**625\.  Sāsa-disato tassa riṭṭho ca** (1170) \[V 117\].

	**Sāsa disa** icc etehi dhātūhi parassa **ta**ppaccayassa **riṭṭhā**deso hoti, **ca**saddena **ti**ssa **riṭṭhi** ca, **disa**to kicca**ta**kāra-**tuṃ-tvā**dīnañ ca **raṭṭha-raṭṭhuṃ-raṭṭhā**desā ca honti,[^340] **r**ādilopo, anusiṭṭho so mayā,[^341] anusiṭṭhā sā, anusiṭṭhaṃ. **Disa** pekkhaṇe, adissitthā ti diṭṭhaṃ me rūpaṃ.[^342]

	**Ti**mhi: anusāsanaṃ anusiṭṭhi, dassanaṃ diṭṭhi.[^343]

	**Kiccā**dīsu: dassanīyaṃ daṭṭhabbaṃ, daṭṭheyyaṃ, passitun ti daṭṭhuṃ gacchati, passitvā ti nekkhamaṃ daṭṭhuṃ,[^344] daṭṭhā,[^345] **i**kārāgamena antaritassa na bhavati, yathā: anusāsitaṃ, anusāsitabbaṃ, anusāsituṃ, anusāsitvā, dassitaṃ icc ādi.

	**Tusa** pītimhi, atussī ti atthe kattari **ta**ppaccayo.

	“Tassā” ti adhikāro.

	**626\. Sādi santa-puccha-bhanja-hansādīhi ṭṭho** (1176) \[V 140, 143\].

	Ādinā saha vattatī ti **sādi**. **Sa**kārantehi, **puccha bhanja hansa** icc evamādīhi ca dhātūhi parassa anantaritassa **ta**kārassa sahādibyañjanena dhātvantena **ṭṭhā**deso hoti. **Hansa**ssa sati pi santatte puna gahaṇaṃ kvaci **ṭṭhā**desassa aniccatādīpanatthaṃ, tena “viddhasto utrasto” ti ādīsu na hoti.[^346] Tuṭṭho,[^347] santusito. **Bhassa** bhassane, abhassī ti bhaṭṭho, bhassito. **Nasa** adassane, nassī ti naṭṭho.[^348] **Daṃsa** daṃsane, adaṃsīyitthā ti daṭṭho sappena,[^349] ḍaṃsito vā, “kvaci dhātū” ti ādinā **da**ssa **ḍa**ttaṃ. **Phusa** phassane, apusīyitthā ti phuṭṭho rogena,[^350] phussito vā. **Isu** icchāyaṃ, esīyitthā ti iṭṭho,[^351] icchito, esito. **Masa** āmasane, āmasīyitthā ti āmaṭṭho. **Vasa** secane, avassī ti vuṭṭho[^352] devo, pavisīyitthā ti paviṭṭho,[^353] uddisīyitthā ti uddiṭṭho.[^354] **Puccha** pucchane, apucchīyitthā ti[^355] puṭṭho pañhaṃ, pucchito. **Bhanja** avamaddane,[^356] abhañjīyitthā ti bhaṭṭhaṃ dhaññaṃ. **Hansa** pītimhi, ahaṃsī ti haṭṭho,[^357] pahaṭṭho, pahaṃsito.

	**Ādi**saddena **yaja** devapūjā-saṅgatikaraṇa-dānesu, ijjitthā ti atthe **ta**ppaccayo, **ta**ssa **ṭṭhā**deso.

	**627\. Yajassa sarass’ i ṭṭhe** (1215) \[V 113\].

	**Yaja** icc etassa dhātussa sarassa **i**kārādeso hoti **ṭṭhe** pare. Yiṭṭho[^358] mayā jino. **Saja** vissagge **saṃ**pubbo, saṃsajjitthā ti saṃsaṭṭho[^359] tena, vissaṭṭho. **Maja** suddhimhi, amajjī ti maṭṭho[^360] icc ādi.

	Kicca**ta**kārādīsu tussitabbaṃ toṭṭhabbaṃ,[^361] phusitabbaṃ phoṭṭhabbaṃ,[^362] pucchituṃ puṭṭhuṃ, yajituṃ yiṭṭhuṃ, abhiharituṃ abhihaṭṭhuṃ, tosanaṃ tuṭṭhi, esanaṃ eṭṭhi, vassanaṃ vuṭṭhi, vissajjanaṃ vissaṭṭhi icc ādi.

	“Tassa,” “sādī” ti ca vattate.

	**628\. Bhanjato ggo ca** (1180) \[V 154\].

	**Bhanja**to dhātumhā **ta**ppaccayassa sahādibyañjanena **ggo** ādeso hoti. Bhaggo rāgo anena.[^363] **Vasa** nivāse, parivasīyitthā ti parivuṭṭho parivāso, vusitaṃ brahmacariyaṃ,[^364] **uṭṭha-u**\-ādesā. **Vasa** acchādane, nivasīyitthā ti nivatthaṃ vatthaṃ, “kvaci dhātū” ti ādinā **sta**kārasaṃyogassa **ttha**ttaṃ, evaṃ nivatthabbaṃ. **Saṃsa** pasaṃsane, pasaṃsīyitthā ti pasattho[^365] pasaṃsito, pasaṃsanaṃ pasatthi. **Badha** bandhane, abajjhitthā ti baddho[^366] raññā, alabhīyitthā ti laddhaṃ[^367] me dhanaṃ, **dha**tta-**da**ttāni. **Rabha** rābhasse, ārabhīyitthā ti āraddhaṃ[^368] vīriyaṃ. **Daha** bhasmīkaraṇe, adayhitthā ti daḍḍhaṃ[^369] vanaṃ, abhujjitthā ti bhutto odano, bhujādittā dhātvantalopo, dvittañ ca. **Caja** hānimhi, pariccajīyitthā ti pariccattaṃ[^370] dhanaṃ, amuccitthā ti mutto[^371] saro.

	**Vaca** viyattiyaṃ vācāyaṃ, avacīyitthā ti atthe **ta**ppaccayo.

	“Anto,” “no,” “dvi,” “cā” ti ca adhikāro.

	**629\. Vaca vā v’ u** (1182) \[V 110-11\].

**Putha**iccetassa dhātussa **puthu patha**iccete ādesā honti, **ama**ppaccayo ca hoti vā, kvacatthoyaṃ **vā**saddo. **Putha** vitthāre, patthaṭāti atthe **kvi**ppaccayo, iminā **putha**ssa**puthupathā**desā, **kvi**lopo. **Itthiyaṃ ī**paccayo, **‘‘o** sare cā’’ti sutte **ca**saddena **avā**deso, puthavī, pathavī, padhavī, **tha**ssa **va**ttaṃ, **ama**ppaccaye **pathā**deso, pathamo.

**677**.**Sasvādīhi tu-davo**.

**Sasu** icc evamādīhi dhātūhi **tu du** icc ete paccayā honti. **Sasu** hiṃsāgatīsu, **tu**ppaccayo, ‘‘kvaci dhātū’’tiādinā dhātvantassa **ta**kāro, sattu. **Jana** janane, jattu. **Dada** dāne, daddu kuṭṭhaviseso. **Ada** bhakkhaṇe, addu. **Mada**ummāde, maddu iccādi.

**678**.**Jhādīhiīvaro**.

**Cipādhā**iccevamādīhi dhātūhi **īvara**ppaccayo hoti. **Ci** caye, cīyatī ti cīvaraṃ. **Pā** pāne, pātīti pīvaro pīno. **Dhā** dhāraṇe, dhīvaro kevaṭṭo.

**679**.**Munādīhi c’ i**.

**Munā**dīhi dhātūhi **i**ppaccayo hoti, **ca**saddena pāṭipadikehi ca. **Muna** ñāṇe, munātī ti muni, **vā**dhikārā na vuddhi. **Yata** yatane, yatatīti yati. **Agga** gatikoṭille, aggi. **Pata** gatimhi, pati. **Suca** socakammani, suci. **Ruca** dittimhi, ruci. **Isa** pariyesane, sīlādiguṇe esatīti isi. **Ku**sadde, kavi, vuddhi, **avā**deso ca. **Ru** sadde, ravi, dadhi, kuṭi. **Asu** khepane, asi. **Rāja**dittimhi, rāji. **Gapu, sappa ga**timhi, sappi. **Acca** pūjāyaṃ, acci. **Juta** dittimhi, joti, nandi, dīpi, kimi, **a**kārassa **i**ttaṃ. **Tamu** kaṅkhāyaṃ, timi. **Budha** bodhane, bujjhatīti bodhi. **Kasa**vilekhane, kasi. **Kapi** calane, kapi, kali, bali, masi, dhani, hari, ari, giri iccādayo.

Pāṭipadikato pana mahāli, bhaddāli, maṇi, araṇi, taraṇi, dharaṇi, saraṇi, dhamaṇi, avani, asani, vasani iccādi.

**680**.**Vidādīhyūro**.

**Vida** iccevamādīhi dhātūhi **ūra**ppaccayo hoti. **Vida** lābhe, vandituṃ alaṃ anāsannattāti atthe **ūra**ppaccayo. Vidūro, vijjūro vā, vidūre jāto vedūro maṇi. **Vala,valla**sādhāraṇabandhanesu, vallūro. **Masa** āmasane, masūro. **Sida** siṅgāre, sindūro, niggahītāgamo. **Du gati**mhi, dūro. **Ku** sadde, kūro. **Kapu** hiṃsātakkalagandhesu, kappūro, dvittaṃ. **Maya** gatimhi, mayūro, mahiyaṃ ravatīti vā mayūroti.

‘‘Vaṇṇāgamo vaṇṇavipariyayo ca,  
Dve cāpare vaṇṇavikāranāsā;  
Dhātussa catthātisayena yogo,  
Taduccate pañcavidhaṃ nirutta’’nti –

Vuttaniruttilakkhaṇānusārena ‘‘tesu vuddhī’’tiādinā, ‘‘kvaci dhātū’’tiādinā ca rūpasiddhi veditabbā.

**Udi** pasavanakledanesu, unditumalaṃ samatthoti undūro. **Khajja** bhakkhaṇe, khādituṃ alanti khajjūro. **Kura** akkose, akkositumalanti kurūro. **Su** hiṃsāyaṃ, sūro.

**681**.**Hanādīhi ṇunutavo**.

**Hana**iccevamādīhi dhātūhi **ṇu nu tu** icc ete paccayā honti. **Ṇu**ppaccaye **hana**hiṃsāgatīsu, hanatīti haṇu. **Jana** janane, jāyatīti jāṇu, dhātvantalopo, dīgho. **Bhā** dittimhi, bhātīti bhāṇu. **Ri** santāne, rayatīti reṇu rajo. **Khanu** avadāraṇe, khanti, khaññatīti vā khāṇu.**Ama** gatyādīsu, amatīti aṇu, dhātvantalopo.

**Nu**ppaccaye – **ve** tantasantāne, vāyatīti venu, veṇu vā. **Dhe** pāne, dhāyati vacchaṃ pāyetīti dhenu, bhātīti bhānu.

**Tu**ppaccaye – **dhā** dhāraṇe, kriyaṃ, lakkhaṇaṃ vā dhāretī ti dhātu. **Si** bandhane, sīyati bandhīyatīti setu. **Kī** dhanaviyoge, **ki** unnatimhi, uddhaṃ gacchatīti ketu. **Hi** gatimhi, hinotīti hetu. **Jana** janane, jāyatīti jantu. **Tanu** vitthāre, tanotīti tantu. **Vasa** nivāse, vasati ettha phalaṃ tadāyattavuttitāyāti vatthu, ‘‘kvaci dhātū’’tiādinā sa**ta**kārasaṃyogassa **tthā**deso.

**682**.**Kuṭādīhiṭho**.

**Kuṭā**dīhi dhātūhi **ṭha**ppaccayo hoti. **Kuṭa** chedane, kuṭati chindatīti kuṭṭho byādhi.**Kusa** chedanapūraṇagandhesu, kusatīti koṭṭho udaraṃ, dhātvantalopadvittāni.**Kaṭa**maddane, kaṭati maddatīti kaṭṭhaṃ. **Kaṇa** nimīlane, kaṇṭho.

**683**.**Manupūrasuṇādīhi ussanusisā**.

**Manu pūra suṇa** icc evamādīhi dhātūhi, pāṭipadikehi ca **ussa nusa isa** icc ete paccayā honti.

**Manu** bodhane, ussa nusā, manate jānātī ti manusso, mānuso vā, dhātvantassa **ā**ttaṃ.

**Pūra** dānapūraṇesu, pūratīti puriso, rassattaṃ, poso, **ra**kā**ri**kārānaṃ lopo, vuddhi ca, pure ucce ṭhāne setīti puriso.

**Suṇa** hiṃsākulasandhānesu, suṇati kulaṃ sandahatīti suṇisā. **Ku** kucchite, kavīyatīti karīsaṃ malaṃ, **ku**ssa **kara**ttaṃ, dīgho ca. **Su** hiṃsāyaṃ, andhakāravidhamanena sattānaṃ bhayaṃ hiṃsatīti sūriyo, **ra**kārāgamo, **sa**kārassa **ya**ttañca. **Maha** pūjāyaṃ, mahatīti mahiso, mahiyaṃ setītipi mahiso. **Si** bandhane, sīyati bandhīyatīti sīsaṃ iccādi.

**684**.**Akkharehi kāra**.

**Akkharehi** akkharavācakehi vaṇṇehi **kāra**ppaccayo hoti. **Taddhitā**disutte**ca**ggahaṇena nāmabyapadese **syā**dyuppatti, akāro, akāraṃ, akārena iccādi, ākāro, okāro, kakāro, yakāro, hakāro, ḷakāro. Evakārādīsu pana karīyati uccārīyatīti kāro saddo, eva ca so kāro cāti evakāro. Evaṃ dhikāro, huṃkāro, sādhukāro.

Ikāro dhātuniddese, vikaraṇandhitoti ca;  
Bhavantettha gamissādi, hanatyādīti ñāpakā.

Uṇādippaccayantanayo.

Tabbādī ṇādayo niṭṭhā, tave tunādayo tathā;  
Mānantādi uṇādīti, chaddhā kitakasaṅgaho.

Iti padarūpasiddhiyaṃ kibbidhānakaṇḍo

Sattamo.

**Nigamana**

Sandhi nāmaṃ kārakañca, samāso taddhitaṃ tathā;  
Ākhyātaṃ kitakaṃ kaṇḍā, sattime **rūpasiddhiyaṃ**.

Tedhā sandhiṃ catuddhā padamapi catudhā pañcadhā nāmikañca,  
Byāsā chakkārakaṃ chassamasanamapi chabbhedato taddhitañca;  
Ākhyātaṃ aṭṭhadhā chabbidhamapi kitakaṃ paccayānaṃ pabhedā,

Vikhyātā**nandathera**vhayavaragurunaṃ tambapaṇṇiddhajānaṃ,

Soyaṃ **buddhappiya**vho yati imamujukaṃ **rūpasiddhiṃ a**kāsi.
