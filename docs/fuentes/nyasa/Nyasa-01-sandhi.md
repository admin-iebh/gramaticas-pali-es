# Nyāsa — Sandhi-Kappa (§1–§51 en esta edición)

Nyāsa Pāṭha

Namo Tassa Bhagavato Arahato Sammāsambuddhassa

**Sandhi**

Buddhaṃ visuddham avisuddhajanassa Suddhi-

"Pūjā ca pūjanīyānaṃ, etaṃ maṅgalam uttaman" ti,

"Satt' ime bhikkhave aparihānīyā dhammā. Katame satta? Satthu­gāravatā, Dhammagāravatā, Saṃghagāravatā, sikkhāgāravatā, samādhi­gāravatā, kalyāṇamittatā, sovacassatā" ti.

Atha vā tass' āyuḍḍhanato, vaḍḍhitāyuno ca yathāpaṭiññātatthā­bhinipphādanasamatthatāsambhavato. Vuttañ hi Bhagavatā:

"Abhivādanasīlissa, niccaṃ vuḍḍh'āpacāyino.

Ekamattā rassā ti hi vutte saṅkhyāvasena niyametvā vuttattā ye diyaḍḍhamattikā te asaṅgahitā bhaveyyuṃ, tasmā lahumattā tayo rassā ti saṅkhyāvasena aniyametvā vuttaṃ. Dīghaṃ kālaṃ dīghaṃ, ih' āpi takkālikā tabbohārena dīghā ti vuttā, tam etesam atthī ti vā dīghā. Garusaññākaraṇapayojanaṃ pana vuttanayam eva. Dvimattā dīghā ti avatvā aññe dīghā ti vacanaṃ tiyaḍḍhamattikānam pi saṅgahaṇatthan ti daṭṭhabbaṃ. Rassasaññāy' ānantataṃ pana dīghasaññā 'va vattabbā ti ih' edaṃ vuttaṃ. Vaggā ti ekaṃ padaṃ, pañcapañcaso ti ekaṃ padaṃ, mantā ti ekaṃ padan ti tipadam idaṃ suttaṃ. Makārapariyantā byañjanā pañcapañcavibhāvena ta-d-akkharavanto vā vaggā nāmā ti attho. Ettha ca pañcapañcā ti vicchāvacanaṃ yathā "Gāmo gāmo ramaṇīyo" ti. Iminā pañca hutvā vaggasaññaṃ paṭilabhantānaṃ bahuttaṃ dasseti. So ti vibhāgatthavācako paccayo. "Vibhāge dhā c" eti sutte **ca**-saddena sambhūto, ass' atthī ti vācako vā, tadā "Tad ass' atthī ti vī c" eti ettha **ca**-saddena sambhūto.

(8**) Aṃ iti niggahītaṃ**. Kimattham idam uccate? "Aṃ byañjane niggahītam" icc' evamādīsu niggahītaṃ nām' idan ti ñāpanatthaṃ. Aṃ iti ekaṃ padaṃ, itī ti ekaṃ padaṃ, niggahītan ti ekaṃ padan ti tipadam idaṃ suttaṃ. Aṃ iti yaṃ vuttaṃ taṃ niggahītaṃ nāmā ti attho. Nissāya gayhatī ti niggahītaṃ, kiṃ nissāyā ti ? ce. Saraṃ nissāya, aññesam pi taṃsabhāve sati tannāmālābe parihāro vuttanayo 'va. Ettha ca **iti**-saddena anantare vuttaṃ binduṃ nidassti, niyamābhāve sati pi sarena vinā niddisitum asakkuṇeyyattā laddhasaññena paṭhamaṃ niddiṭṭhena akārena niddiṭṭhabinduṃ va nidassetī ti daṭṭhabbaṃ. Aṃ niggahītan ti vutte pi siddhe **iti**-saddena tam eva atthaṃ pakāseti, saddādhikā atthādhiko hotīt i, taṃ pana upari yeva āvibhavissati. Pacchā niddiṭṭhānaṃ pacchā vattabbato ih' edaṃ vuttaṃ. Idam pi saññāsuttan ti daṭṭhabbaṃ.

(9**) Parasamaññā payoge.** Kimattham idam uccate? "Vagge ghosā' ghosānaṃ tatiyapaṭhamā, "Gahass' upadhass' evā" ti evamādīsu ghos'ā­ghosupadhādayo ime ti ñāpanatthaṃ. Parasamaññā ti ekaṃ padaṃ, payoge ti ekaṃ padan ti dvipadam idaṃ suttaṃ. Paresam pi samaññ yuttaṭṭhāne yojetabbā ti attho. Paresaṃ samaññā parasamaññā, veyyākaraṇānaṃ samaññā ti attho. Parasmiṃvā samaññā parasamaññā, Sakkataganthe samaññā ti attho. Payujjanaṃ payogo, tasmiṃ payoge satī ti sambandho. Attano sāmaññaṃ vatvā parasamaññāya pacchā vattabbato ih' edaṃ vuttaṃ. Tividhā hi saññā anvatthasaññā sakasaññā parasaññā ti. Tā pana akkhara­vaggaghosasaññādivasena dīpetabbā.

ekaṃ padaṃ, viyojaye ti ekaṃ padan ti pañcapadam idaṃ suttaṃ. Pubbabhūtaṃ byañjanaṃ adhoṭhitam assaraṃ katvā sarato viyojaye ti attho. Sarabyañjanapadesu viggaho vutto 'va. Sarato byañjanaṃ viyojaye ti avatvā kasmā evaṃ vuttan? ti. Yadi pan' evaṃ vuttaṃ bhaveyya sasīlavā ti ādīsu sosīlavā ti padacchede kate kataraṃ byañjanaṃ sarato viyojaye ti sandaho jāyeyya pubbaparan ti niyamābhāvā, taṃsandhehanivattanatthaṃ pubba­ggahaṇaṃ kataṃ, pubbe ṭhitaṃ byañjanaṃ sarato viyojetvā kattha ṭhapetabban? ti sandeho jāyeyya, taṃsandehavivattanatthaṃ adhoṭhita­ggahaṇaṃ kataṃ. Āha ca vuttiyaṃ: "Pubbabyañjanaṃ adhoṭhitam assaraṃ katvā" ti. Ayañ h' etth' attho: Adhoṭhitaṃ katvā assaraṃ katvā ti. Assaraggahaṇaṃ pana kasmā kataṃ? Asati pi assaraggahaṇe sarena viyojaye ti vuttattā 'va ta-d-attho viññāyatī? ti. Saccaṃ, uttarasuttattham assaraggahaṇaṃ. Ayañ h' etth' attho: Adhoṭhitaṃ assaraṃ byañjanṃ parakkharaṃ naye ti. Evañ hi sati kasmā tatth' eva na vuttan? ti codanā. Ih 'āpi kiñcimattatthasambavato. Asati hi assaraggahaṇe sarene ti evaṃ paṭhitattā kiṃ pubbabyañjanaṃ adhoṭhitaṃ katvā sarena saha viyojetabban ti sandeho jāyeyya, assaraggahaṇe sati assaraṃ katvā sarena sahā ti atthassa ayuttattā sarene ti ettha sarato ti attho labbhati. Kiṃ nām' etaṃ? Viyojaye ti vuttattā 'va ta-d-attho pi labbhatī? ti. Na labbhati, "Bandhuss' eva samāgamo" ti ādīsu bandhussa ivā ti chede kate parapadaṃ paṭicca parasakārassa pubbayañjanattā, purimasakārato adhoṭhitaṃ katvā para­padaṃ paṭicca taṃ sarena saha viyojetabban ti atthassa sambhavato. Kasmā pan' ettha sarato ti avatvā evaṃ vuttan? ti. Yena kenaci ākārena sissānaṃ pariṇatuppādanatthaṃ pañcamyantass' āpi evarūpaṃ rūpaṃ bhavissatī ti. Tathā hi sarasaddamhā smāvacanassa "Amhatumhantu­rājabrahmatta­sakhasatthupitādīhi smā nā 've" ti sutte **ādi**-ggahaṇena nābhāve kate "Ato ne nāti tassa enādese kate saralopomādesapaccayādimhi saralopetupakatīti pubbasarassa lope kate "Sarā sare lopaṃ", "Vā paro asarūpā" tyadhikicca

"Kvac' āsavaṇṇaṃ lutte" ti parasarassa asavaṇṇe sampatte ten' eva pakati­bhāve kate pubbakkharaṃ parakkharam upanīte evarūpaṃ rūpaṃ hotī ti. Ayaṃ pana karuṇā yevā parādho, n' ācariyassa, tāy' evam akāsī ti. Assaran ti ettha ca akāro virahattho. Idaṃ pana paribhāsāsuttan ti daṭṭhabbaṃ. Tatr' āyami ti udāharanaṃ. (13) **Vā paro asarūpā.** Kimattham idam uccate? Asarūpamhā saramhā paro saro vikappena lopaṃ pappotī ti ñāpanatthaṃ. Vā ti ekaṃ padaṃ, paro ti ekaṃ padaṃ. Asarūpā ti ekaṃ padan ti tipadam idaṃ suttaṃ. Anantarasuttato **sarā**-ggahaṇaṃ "Atthavasā vibattivipariṇāmo" ti katvā ekavacanantena parosaddena sambandho hoti. **Sare**-ggahaṇaṃ pañca­my­antena **asarūpā**-ggahaṇena sambandho hoti.
